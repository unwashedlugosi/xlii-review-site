1979-04-22
Spartan Stadium
214w
APRIL 22, 1979 — SPARTAN STADIUM, SAN JOSE STATE UNIVERSITY. AN OUTDOOR DAYTIME SHOW ON A COLLEGE FOOTBALL FIELD, WITH PLANES FROM THE NEARBY SAN JOSE AIRPORT BUZZING OVERHEAD. THE GREG KIHN BAND AND CHARLIE DANIELS BAND OPENED — DANIELS PLAYED "THE DEVIL WENT DOWN TO GEORGIA" FOUR DAYS BEFORE THE SINGLE WAS EVEN RELEASED. THREE MILE ISLAND WAS LESS THAN A MONTH OLD. AND THEN THE DEAD WALKED ON WITH A NEW FACE BEHIND THE KEYBOARDS — A TWENTY-SIX-YEAR-OLD FROM ANTIOCH WITH CLASSICAL TRAINING AND A TOP-20 POP HIT ALREADY ON HIS RESUME. GARCIA FORGOT TO INTRODUCE HIM. THE BAND OPENED WITH JACK STRAW AND NEVER LOOKED BACK. WHAT'S STRIKING ABOUT THIS TAPE IS HOW LITTLE ADJUSTMENT THERE IS — THE NEW KEYBOARDS LOCK INTO SUGAREE LIKE THEY'VE BEEN THERE FOR YEARS. RELIX NOTED THE HARMONIES "TOOK ON A NEW POWER AND VITALITY" IMMEDIATELY. DURING LOOKS LIKE RAIN, IT ACTUALLY RAINED LIGHTLY WHILE THE SUN WAS STILL SHINING — MULTIPLE PEOPLE IN THE CROWD NOTICED. THE SECOND SET SCARLET BEGONIAS INTO FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN IS THE FIRST REAL TEST OF WHETHER THIS LINEUP CAN HANDLE THE BIG JAMS, AND THE ANSWER IS UNAMBIGUOUS. THE DOUBLE ENCORE — U.S. BLUES FOLLOWED BY SHAKEDOWN STREET — SENT 18,000 PEOPLE HOME KNOWING SOMETHING HAD CHANGED.
1979-05-05
Baltimore Civic Center
192w
MAY 5, 1979 — BALTIMORE CIVIC CENTER, BALTIMORE, MARYLAND. THE CIVIC CENTER WAS BUILT ON THE SITE WHERE THE CONTINENTAL CONGRESS MET IN 1776, THOUGH BY 1979 IT LOOKED LIKE A BETTER-GRADE GYMNASIUM LINED WITH MASONRY WALLS. THE BEATLES, JAMES BROWN, ELVIS, AND LED ZEPPELIN HAD ALL PLAYED HERE. THIS WAS THE FIFTH SHOW WITH THE NEW LINEUP, AND THE BAND WAS STILL FEELING ITS WAY THROUGH THE CATALOG WITH A NEW SET OF HANDS ON THE KEYS. GARCIA WAS FIGHTING LARYNGITIS THROUGH MUCH OF THE SPRING — YOU CAN HEAR A BIT OF IT HERE, BUT IT DOESN'T SLOW HIM DOWN. THE SUGAREE RUNS ABOUT FOURTEEN MINUTES AND BUILDS PATIENTLY, WITH VOCAL AND GUITAR FILLS LAYERING ON TOP OF EACH OTHER. THE SCARLET BEGONIAS INTO FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN TAKES ITS TIME WITH THE TRANSITION — ONE LISTENER DESCRIBED IT AS "SLOW AND GRACEFUL," THE KIND THAT DEVELOPS OVER WHAT FEELS LIKE FOREVER. THE OTHER ONE REACHES BACK INTO THE AGGRESSIVE TERRITORY OF 1973, WITH TWISTS AND TURNS THAT SHOW THE NEW GUY COULD FOLLOW THE BAND INTO UNCHARTED SPACE. A SATURDAY NIGHT SHOW WITH A SATURDAY NIGHT ENCORE TO MATCH.
1979-08-04
Oakland Auditorium Arena
185w
AUGUST 4, 1979 — OAKLAND AUDITORIUM ARENA, OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA. A 1914 BEAUX-ARTS BUILDING NEAR LAKE MERRITT WITH FADED ART DECO ELEGANCE INSIDE — BILL GRAHAM HAD STARTED BOOKING SHOWS HERE AFTER WINTERLAND CLOSED, AND THIS NIGHT CEMENTED IT AS THE DEAD'S NEW HOME COURT. THREE THINGS THAT WOULD DEFINE THE NEXT DECADE ALL APPEARED ON THE SAME STAGE: A THIRTEEN-AND-A-HALF-POUND GUITAR MADE OF COCOBOLO, MAPLE, AND PADAUK THAT TOOK ITS BUILDER SIX YEARS AND TWO THOUSAND HOURS TO FINISH. A SONG CALLED ALTHEA THAT WOULD LATER BE RANKED THE FIFTH BEST DEAD SONG EVER BY STEREOGUM. AND LOST SAILOR, WHICH ARRIVED WITHOUT ITS SOON-TO-BE-PERMANENT PARTNER SAINT OF CIRCUMSTANCE — THAT WOULDN'T DEBUT UNTIL THE END OF THE MONTH AT GLENS FALLS. BILLBOARD REPORTED 13,000 TICKETS SOLD ACROSS THIS TWO-NIGHT STAND, GROSSING $110,500. THE CROWD ON THE GENERAL ADMISSION FLOOR PACKED THE PLACE TO ITS ROUGHLY 7,000 CAPACITY. FOUR MONTHS INTO THE NEW LINEUP, THE CHEMISTRY WAS NOW LOCKED IN. THE PLAYING IN THE BAND IN SET TWO IS PROOF — ORGAN AND HARMONY VOCALS FILLING SPACES THAT HAD BEEN HOLLOW FOR THE LAST TWO YEARS.
1979-09-02
Augusta Civic Center
201w
SEPTEMBER 2, 1979 — AUGUSTA CIVIC CENTER, AUGUSTA, MAINE. THE FIRST TIME THE DEAD PLAYED THE STATE CAPITAL — A SMALL CITY OF 22,000 PEOPLE, THREE HOURS NORTH OF BOSTON WITH NO DIRECT INTERSTATE CONNECTION. THE CIVIC CENTER HAD OPENED IN 1973 ON LAND DEEDED TO THE CITY FOR ONE DOLLAR. IT WAS THE ONLY MAINE VENUE THAT EVER HOSTED ELVIS PRESLEY, WHO PLAYED HERE IN MAY 1977 AND WAS SUPPOSED TO RETURN IN AUGUST BUT DIED FIRST. LABOR DAY WEEKEND, A SUNDAY SHOW. COLLEGE STUDENTS WERE ARRIVING BACK IN NEW ENGLAND AND SOME OF THEM DROVE THE HALF HOUR FROM BOWDOIN COLLEGE. ONE OF THOSE STUDENTS RECALLED THAT ONCE TERRAPIN STATION OPENED THE SECOND SET, "THE SHOW REALLY GOT INTO GEAR." THE FIRST SET RUNS TWELVE SONGS DEEP — THE BAND WAS IN A GIVING MOOD, WORKING THROUGH THE GARCIA AND HUNTER SONGBOOK WITH THE LOOSE CONFIDENCE OF A GROUP THAT HAD SPENT THE SUMMER GETTING TIGHT. NONE OF THE NEW SONGS DEBUTED OVER THE SUMMER — ALTHEA, LOST SAILOR, SAINT OF CIRCUMSTANCE — APPEAR HERE. THE BAND STUCK TO WHAT THEY KNEW AND PLAYED IT WELL. THE DEAD WOULD RETURN TO AUGUSTA FOUR MORE TIMES OVER THE NEXT DECADE.
1979-10-25
New Haven Coliseum
209w
OCTOBER 25, 1979 — NEW HAVEN VETERANS MEMORIAL COLISEUM, NEW HAVEN, CONNECTICUT. THE COLISEUM WAS A BRUTALIST CONCRETE MONUMENT TO 1970S CIVIC AMBITION — THE PARKING GARAGE SAT ON TOP OF THE ARENA BECAUSE THE HIGH WATER TABLE MADE UNDERGROUND PARKING IMPOSSIBLE. DRIVERS SPIRALED UP A QUARTER-MILE CORKSCREW RAMP TO REACH THE ROOF. YALE ARCHITECTURAL HISTORIAN VINCENT SCULLY CALLED IT "STRUCTURAL EXHIBITIONISM." THE YALE DAILY NEWS WAS MORE DIRECT: "UGLY BUT OURS." SECOND SHOW OF A FOURTEEN-DATE FALL EAST COAST TOUR. THREE DAYS EARLIER THE SHAH OF IRAN HAD BEEN ADMITTED TO A NEW YORK HOSPITAL — TEN DAYS LATER, THE CONSEQUENCES WOULD ARRIVE. THE SHAKEDOWN STREET THAT OPENS SET TWO IS THE CONSENSUS HIGHLIGHT — LONG, BOUNCY, AND TIGHT ENOUGH TO EARN A SPOT ON ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 1, NUMBER 1, THE INAUGURAL RELEASE IN THAT SERIES. THE FALL 1979 TOUR WAS CHOSEN TO LAUNCH THE ENTIRE ROAD TRIPS CATALOG — A SIGNAL OF HOW GOOD THIS STRETCH OF SHOWS WOULD GET. FRIEND OF THE DEVIL SHOWS UP IN SET TWO, WHERE THE SLOWER TEMPO AND DARKER ROOM GIVE IT A DIFFERENT CHARACTER THAN ITS USUAL FIRST-SET HOME. THE COLISEUM WAS IMPLODED IN 2007 WITH TWO THOUSAND POUNDS OF EXPLOSIVES. TWENTY THOUSAND PEOPLE CAME TO WATCH IT DIE.
1979-10-27
Cape Cod Coliseum
215w
OCTOBER 27, 1979 — CAPE COD COLISEUM, SOUTH YARMOUTH, MASSACHUSETTS. A CONCRETE ICE HOCKEY BARN OFF ROUTE 6, BUILT IN 1972 FOR A MILLION AND A HALF DOLLARS. HOME OF THE CAPE COD CUBS UNTIL THEY FOLDED IN 1977. NO AIR CONDITIONING. TERRIBLE ACOUSTICS — THE WALLS RATTLED AND THE SOUND BOOMED. TICKETS WERE EIGHT FIFTY. CAPE COD IN LATE OCTOBER IS OFF-SEASON QUIET, AND THE DEAD PLAYED THE BEST SHOW OF THEIR YEAR HERE. THE FRANKLIN'S TOWER IN SET TWO RUNS PAST SEVENTEEN MINUTES, WITH GARCIA DELIVERING SOLOS SO LIQUID AND SO FAST THAT THE ROOM COULDN'T KEEP UP. BUT THE REAL PRIZE IS WHAT HAPPENS AFTER HE'S GONE: THE BAND DROPS INTO THE LAST CAUTION (DO NOT STOP ON TRACKS) JAM THEY WOULD EVER PLAY, AND THEN PHIL LESH HITS A BASS DROP INTO THE OTHER ONE THAT ONE LISTENER DESCRIBED AS "A SEA OF SERPENTS." THE DEAD PLAYED CAPE COD EXACTLY TWICE — THIS NIGHT AND THE NEXT. NOBODY CAME BACK. IN 1979, VINCE MCMAHON BOUGHT THE BUILDING FOR WRESTLING. THE LAST EVENT WAS A WWF SHOW IN 1984. CHRISTMAS TREE SHOPS TURNED IT INTO A WAREHOUSE, WHICH IT STILL IS. SELECTED FOR THE 30 TRIPS AROUND THE SUN BOX SET AS THE REPRESENTATIVE 1979 SHOW FROM THE DEAD'S ENTIRE THIRTY-YEAR CAREER.
1979-10-31
Nassau Coliseum
201w
OCTOBER 31, 1979 — NASSAU VETERANS MEMORIAL COLISEUM, UNIONDALE, NEW YORK. HALLOWEEN NIGHT, OPENING A THREE-SHOW RUN AT THE DEAD'S MOST-VISITED ARENA ON LONG ISLAND — THEY'D PLAY NASSAU FORTY-TWO TIMES BEFORE IT WAS OVER. THE COLISEUM SAT ON SIXTY-THREE ACRES OF MITCHEL FIELD, A FORMER ARMY AND AIR FORCE BASE. THE NEW YORK ISLANDERS WERE IN THE MIDDLE OF BUILDING A DYNASTY — FOUR STRAIGHT STANLEY CUPS STARTING THE FOLLOWING SPRING — AND THE HOCKEY RINK BOARDS MAY STILL HAVE BEEN UP AROUND THE FLOOR. COSTUMES IN THE CROWD. CHINA CAT SUNFLOWER INTO I KNOW YOU RIDER OPENED THE SHOW, WHICH IS AS FESTIVE A SIGNAL AS A BAND CAN SEND. ALTHEA AND LOST SAILOR INTO SAINT OF CIRCUMSTANCE WERE BOTH STILL NEW ENOUGH TO CARRY AN ELECTRIC CHARGE — ALTHEA HAD DEBUTED LESS THAN THREE MONTHS EARLIER. THE SHAKEDOWN STREET IS ONE OF THE BEST FROM THE 1970S, WITH THE KEYBOARDS GETTING FUNKY IN WAYS THE PREVIOUS OCCUPANT NEVER ATTEMPTED. THE SHOW HAS LIVED IN THE SHADOW OF WHAT CAME TWO DAYS BEFORE AT CAPE COD AND WHAT CAME THE NEXT TWO NIGHTS, BUT IT HOLDS UP. MATERIAL FROM THIS DATE APPEARS ON ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 1, NUMBER 1.
1979-11-01
Nassau Coliseum
188w
NOVEMBER 1, 1979 — NASSAU VETERANS MEMORIAL COLISEUM, UNIONDALE, NEW YORK. NIGHT TWO OF THREE. THE SCARLET BEGONIAS INTO FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN FROM THIS SHOW MAY BE THE LONGEST THE BAND EVER PLAYED THAT PAIRING — OVER THIRTY MINUTES OF CONTINUOUS MUSIC, WITH A JAM AND TRANSITION SECTION THAT BUILDS WITH A PATIENCE THE DEAD RARELY MATCHED IN THIS ERA. IT WAS DEEMED GOOD ENOUGH TO HIDE ON AN OFFICIAL RELEASE: AFTER THE LAST TRACK ON DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 13 — A 1981 NASSAU SHOW — WAIT THIRTY OR FORTY SECONDS OF SILENCE AND THIS ENTIRE SCARLET INTO FIRE STARTS PLAYING. NO SEPARATE TRACK LISTING, NO MENTION IN THE LINER NOTES. THIRTY-FIVE MINUTES OF HIDDEN MUSIC. THE PLAYING IN THE BAND IN SET TWO IS ALSO WORTH THE PRICE OF ADMISSION. THE SONG HAD EIGHT YEARS OF HISTORY BEHIND IT, NONE OF WHICH THE NEW KEYBOARD PLAYER HAD BEEN PART OF, BUT BY THIS POINT — SEVEN MONTHS IN — HE WAS NAVIGATING ITS IMPROVISATIONAL CORRIDORS LIKE HE'D HELPED BUILD THEM. PEGGY-O IN SET ONE IS QUIETLY BEAUTIFUL. THE IRAN HOSTAGE CRISIS WOULD BEGIN IN THREE DAYS.
1979-11-02
Nassau Coliseum
178w
NOVEMBER 2, 1979 — NASSAU VETERANS MEMORIAL COLISEUM, UNIONDALE, NEW YORK. FINAL NIGHT OF THE HALLOWEEN RUN. THE OPENER IS A SURPRISE: MISSISSIPPI HALF-STEP UPTOWN TOODELOO THAT JAMS DEEP AND LONG BEFORE DISSOLVING INTO FRANKLIN'S TOWER — AN UNUSUAL PAIRING THAT CAUGHT THE CROWD OFF GUARD. ONE LISTENER SAID IT "SEEMS TO LAST A LIFETIME." THE NOT FADE AWAY THAT EMERGES FROM DRUMS IS THE BEST OF THE TOUR — BUILT ON SHARED CONTRIBUTION FROM ALL FIVE INSTRUMENTS RATHER THAN GARCIA DOMINATING THE CONVERSATION. LAZY LIGHTNING INTO SUPPLICATION IS PUNCHY AND TIGHT. THE RUN AS A WHOLE REPRESENTS THE FALL 1979 TOUR REACHING FULL STRIDE — FOUR NIGHTS BEHIND THEM AT THIS POINT, EACH ONE BUILDING ON THE LAST. TWO DAYS LATER, IRANIAN STUDENTS WOULD STORM THE AMERICAN EMBASSY IN TEHRAN. TWO DAYS AFTER THAT, THE DEAD PLAYED PROVIDENCE AND DEBUTED ALABAMA GETAWAY. BUT THAT'S ALL AHEAD. THIS NIGHT THE ENCORE IS CASEY JONES AND THE MOOD IS PURE SATURDAY NIGHT ON LONG ISLAND. NOT FADE AWAY FROM THIS DATE APPEARS ON ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 1, NUMBER 1.

The Spectrum
200w
NOVEMBER 6, 1979 — THE SPECTRUM, PHILADELPHIA, PENNSYLVANIA. THE DEAD PLAYED THE SPECTRUM FIFTY-THREE TIMES — MORE THAN ANY OTHER MUSICAL ACT, INCLUDING SPRINGSTEEN. THE BUILDING'S NAME WAS A TONGUE-IN-CHEEK ACRONYM: SP FOR SPORTS AND SOUTH PHILADELPHIA, E FOR ENTERTAINMENT, C FOR CIRCUSES, T FOR THEATRICALS, R FOR RECREATION, UM FOR "UM, WHAT A NICE BUILDING." THE ROOF BLEW OFF TWICE IN ITS FIRST YEAR. JOHNNY CARSON JOKED ABOUT IT ON THE TONIGHT SHOW. THE IRAN HOSTAGE CRISIS WAS TWO DAYS OLD AND DOMINATING EVERY TELEVISION IN AMERICA. ALABAMA GETAWAY OPENS THE SHOW — THE SONG HAD DEBUTED JUST TWO DAYS EARLIER AND WOULD EVENTUALLY REACH NUMBER 68 ON THE BILLBOARD CHART. THE BLACK PETER IN SET TWO IS THE STANDOUT: SLOW, INTENSE, WITH ORGAN PLAYING THAT ONE LISTENER SAID MADE HIM "FEEL LIKE YOU'RE AT A PSYCHEDELIC CHURCH REVIVAL." DURING DRUMS, MICKEY HART HELD A TAR TO THE MICROPHONE AND SCREAMED "YEAH MAN, KEEP GOING!" WHILE THE FEEDBACK HOWLED. APOCALYPSE NOW HAD BEEN IN THEATERS LESS THAN THREE MONTHS — ONE PERSON IN THE CROWD THOUGHT THE DRUMS SECTION "SOUNDS LIKE OUTTAKES FROM THE APOCALYPSE NOW SESSIONS." MATERIAL FROM THIS DATE APPEARS ON ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 1, NUMBER 1.
1979-11-09
Community War Memorial Auditorium
202w
NOVEMBER 9, 1979 — BUFFALO MEMORIAL AUDITORIUM, BUFFALO, NEW YORK. "THE AUD" OPENED IN 1940 AS A WAR MEMORIAL — ITS FIRST EVENT WAS A RALLY FOR WENDELL WILLKIE'S PRESIDENTIAL CAMPAIGN. ONE OF THE CONSTRUCTION WORKERS WAS ROBERT MARELLA, LATER FAMOUS AS PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER GORILLA MONSOON. IN 1965, THE ROLLING STONES PLAYED HERE FOR SEVEN MINUTES BEFORE THOUSANDS RUSHED THE STAGE AND POLICE CANCELLED THE SHOW. THE DEAD HAD A BETTER NIGHT. THIS WAS CALLED THE BEST ALL-AROUND SHOW OF THE FALL TOUR, AND THE ROAD TRIPS RELEASE BACKED THAT UP — NINE SONGS FROM THIS DATE APPEAR ON VOLUME 1, NUMBER 1, MORE THAN ANY OTHER SHOW IN THE COMPILATION. DANCING IN THE STREET INTO FRANKLIN'S TOWER OPENS SET TWO AND, AS ONE WITNESS PUT IT, "NEARLY SPLIT THE FLOOR IN TWO." THE HE'S GONE JAM DRIFTS INTO UNEXPECTED GLORIA RIFFS — VAN MORRISON BY WAY OF OUTER SPACE. THE WHARF RAT IS ONE OF THE BEST OF THE ERA. THE BAND WAS AT A CROSSROADS: OLD-SCHOOL EXPLORATORY DEAD MEETING THE ENERGY OF THE NEW LINEUP, WITH JUST ENOUGH ROUGH EDGES TO KEEP THINGS INTERESTING. FIVE DAYS INTO THE HOSTAGE CRISIS. COLD NOVEMBER IN BUFFALO. THE AUD WAS DEMOLISHED IN 2009.
1979-11-10
Crisler Arena
182w
NOVEMBER 10, 1979 — CRISLER ARENA, UNIVERSITY OF MICHIGAN, ANN ARBOR. THE HOUSE THAT CAZZIE BUILT — NAMED FOR FRITZ CRISLER BUT PROMPTED BY CAZZIE RUSSELL'S BIG TEN BASKETBALL TITLES IN THE MID-SIXTIES, WHICH OUTGREW THE OLD FIELDHOUSE AND FORCED CONSTRUCTION OF THIS 13,000-SEAT ARENA. EIGHT YEARS EARLIER, JOHN LENNON AND YOKO ONO HAD TAKEN THIS STAGE AT THREE IN THE MORNING FOR THE JOHN SINCLAIR FREEDOM RALLY — FIFTEEN THOUSAND PEOPLE, AN EIGHT-HOUR PROGRAM, AND SINCLAIR WAS FREED THREE DAYS LATER. LAST SHOW OF THE MAIN FALL TOUR. THE NIGHT WAS COLD AND RAINY AND THE OPENER — COLD RAIN AND SNOW — FIT THE WEATHER PERFECTLY. FANS HAD CAMPED OUTSIDE FOR FOUR OR FIVE NIGHTS WHEN TICKETS WENT ON SALE. THE TWELVE-SONG FIRST SET FEATURES A RARE HIGH TIME, ONE OF GARCIA'S MOST EMOTIONAL BALLADS. THE PASSENGER IS THE SONG PEOPLE TALK ABOUT FROM THIS TAPE — A "JAM ANTHEM VERSION" WHERE GARCIA'S FINAL SOLO HITS WITH WHAT ONE LISTENER DESCRIBED AS "HURRICANE-FORCE LICKS." THIRTEEN DAYS OFF BEFORE SAN DIEGO. THE TOUR WAS OVER AND THE BAND HAD ANNOUNCED ITSELF.
1979-12-26
Oakland Auditorium
196w
DECEMBER 26, 1979 — OAKLAND AUDITORIUM ARENA, OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA. OPENING NIGHT OF THE FIRST-EVER OAKLAND NEW YEAR'S RUN — FIVE SHOWS OVER SIX DAYS, PICKING UP WHERE WINTERLAND LEFT OFF EXACTLY ONE YEAR EARLIER. THIS WAS A BENEFIT FOR WAVY GRAVY'S SEVA FOUNDATION, A SIGHT-RESTORATION CHARITY CO-FOUNDED BY RAM DASS — THE FORMER HARVARD PSYCHOLOGIST WHO'D TAKEN A DIFFERENT PATH THAN HIS COLLEAGUE TIMOTHY LEARY. THE INAUGURAL SEVA BENEFIT CONCERT. UNCLE JOHN'S BAND RETURNED AFTER A HUNDRED AND SEVENTY SHOWS AND OVER TWENTY-SIX MONTHS AWAY. BROKEDOWN PALACE CAME BACK TOO — A HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE SHOWS SINCE ANYONE HAD HEARD IT. THE BAND PLAYED UNCLE JOHN'S BAND TWICE — ONCE TO OPEN SET TWO AND AGAIN AS THE ENCORE CLOSER, AS IF THEY COULDN'T BELIEVE THEY'D FOUND IT AGAIN. THE ESTIMATED PROPHET RUNS NEARLY NINETEEN MINUTES. THIS FIVE-NIGHT RUN IS WHERE PARKING LOT VENDING — WHAT WOULD BECOME KNOWN AS "SHAKEDOWN STREET" — WAS BORN. FANS CAMPED IN THE PARK NEAR LAKE MERRITT, AND PROMOTER BOB BARSOTTI LET THEM STAY. RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 5 — THE FIRST DICK'S PICKS TO CONTAIN A COMPLETE SHOW, AND THE FIRST OFFICIAL FULL-CONCERT RELEASE OF THE NEW LINEUP.

Oakland Auditorium Arena

DECEMBER 28, 1979 — OAKLAND AUDITORIUM ARENA, OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA. SATURDAY NIGHT, THIRD SHOW OF THE NEW YEAR'S RUN. THE IT'S ALL OVER NOW FROM THIS SHOW IS CONSIDERED THE DEFINITIVE VERSION — THE SINGLE BEST THE DEAD EVER PLAYED, ACCORDING TO TAPER CONSENSUS. THE ROLLING STONES HAD MADE THE SONG FAMOUS FIFTEEN YEARS EARLIER, BUT WHAT HAPPENS HERE BELONGS ENTIRELY TO THIS BAND ON THIS NIGHT. THE TERRAPIN STATION INTO PLAYING IN THE BAND ANCHORS AN EXPLORATORY SECOND SET THAT SHIFTS BETWEEN SPACEY JAMS AND HIGH-ENERGY ROCK AND ROLL. THE DOUBLE ENCORE — CASEY JONES FOLLOWED BY ONE MORE SATURDAY NIGHT — DELIVERS EXACTLY WHAT A SATURDAY CROWD WANTS. RELEASED AS ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 3, NUMBER 1 — A TWO-DISC SET OF THE COMPLETE CONCERT. EARLY COPIES CAME WITH A BONUS THIRD DISC FEATURING MATERIAL FROM THE DECEMBER 30 SHOW TWO NIGHTS LATER. THE LINER NOTES DESCRIBED IT AS "A SUPER HIGH-ENERGY ROCK AND ROLL SHOW THAT ALSO HAS ITS SHARE OF SPACEY JAMS AND EXPLORATORY PASSAGES." THAT'S AS ACCURATE A SUMMARY AS ANYONE HAS WRITTEN.
1979-12-30
Oakland Auditorium Arena
184w
DECEMBER 30, 1979 — OAKLAND AUDITORIUM ARENA, OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA. PENULTIMATE NIGHT OF THE RUN. THE BAND WAS FOUR SHOWS DEEP AND PLAYING WITH THE LOOSENESS THAT COMES FROM KNOWING YOU'RE HOME AND THE ROOM IS YOURS. THE SCARLET BEGONIAS INTO FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN ANCHORS SET TWO. THE LAZY LIGHTNING INTO SUPPLICATION THAT CLOSES SET ONE HITS HARD AND FAST. BUT THE MOMENT PEOPLE REMEMBER IS THE DOUBLE ENCORE: DON'T EASE ME IN — A ROWDY JUG-BAND ROMP — FOLLOWED IMMEDIATELY BY BROKEDOWN PALACE, ONE OF GARCIA'S MOST DEVASTATING BALLADS. THE WHIPLASH IS INTENTIONAL. ONE MINUTE THE ROOM IS A PARTY, AND THE NEXT GARCIA IS SINGING "FARE YOU WELL, FARE YOU WELL" AND THE PARTY IS SOMETHING ELSE ENTIRELY. BROKEDOWN PALACE WAS GETTING ONLY ITS SECOND PERFORMANCE OF THE RUN AFTER RETURNING FROM A TWENTY-SIX-MONTH ABSENCE ON OPENING NIGHT. IT WAS STILL FRAGILE, STILL FINDING ITS LEGS AGAIN, AND THAT UNCERTAINTY MAKES IT HIT HARDER. MATERIAL FROM THIS SHOW APPEARS ON THE BONUS DISC PACKAGED WITH EARLY COPIES OF ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 3, NUMBER 1. NEW YEAR'S EVE WAS TOMORROW. ONE MORE NIGHT.
1979-12-31
Oakland Auditorium Arena
184w
DECEMBER 31, 1979 — OAKLAND AUDITORIUM ARENA, OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA. THE LAST NIGHT OF THE DECADE. BILL GRAHAM'S FIRST NEW YEAR'S EVE AT OAKLAND, REPLACING THE WINTERLAND TRADITION. THE FLYING KARAMAZOV BROTHERS — A SANTA CRUZ JUGGLING TROUPE, NEITHER BROTHERS NOR RUSSIAN — OPENED BY JUGGLING CHAMPAGNE BOTTLES, BOWLING PINS, MEAT CLEAVERS, AND BURNING TORCHES. AS THEIR ACT PEAKED, GARCIA WALKED OUT FROM STAGE LEFT WITH HIS GUITAR AND STROLLED CASUALLY THROUGH THEIR SEMI-CIRCLE OF AIRBORNE OBJECTS. THE BROTHERS NEVER MISSED A BEAT. AT MIDNIGHT, GRAHAM EMERGED FROM A "DEAD HEAD" DELIVERY TRUCK IN A GIANT BUTTERFLY COSTUME WITH LIGHTNING-SKULL WINGS AND FLEW TO THE STAGE ON WIRES BEFORE BEING CAPTURED BY TWO INSECT CHASERS WITH A GIANT NET. THREE FULL SETS. JOHN CIPOLLINA OF QUICKSILVER MESSENGER SERVICE SAT IN FOR NOT FADE AWAY, STELLA BLUE, AND SUNSHINE DAYDREAM IN THE THIRD SET — THE LAST SUNSHINE DAYDREAM THE BAND EVER PERFORMED. WEIR WAS REPORTEDLY SICK WITH THE FLU BY SET THREE. ATTENDEES ACKNOWLEDGED IT "PALED COMPARED TO 1978" BUT NOBODY CARED MUCH — THE DECADE WAS OVER AND A NEW ONE WAS WALKING IN.
1980-01-13
Oakland-Alameda County Coliseum
189w
JANUARY 13, 1980 — OAKLAND-ALAMEDA COUNTY COLISEUM ARENA, OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA. NOT THE AUDITORIUM WHERE THE NEW YEAR'S RUN HAD JUST HAPPENED — THE BIG COLISEUM, THE WARRIORS' BUILDING, FOURTEEN THOUSAND SEATS. JOAN BAEZ ORGANIZED THIS CAMBODIAN REFUGEE BENEFIT AFTER VIETNAM'S INVASION OF CAMBODIA AND THE FALL OF THE KHMER ROUGE HAD FORCED THREE-QUARTERS OF A MILLION PEOPLE TO THE THAI BORDER. THE BILL WAS STACKED: BAEZ, THE BEACH BOYS WITH BRIAN WILSON ON PIANO, JEFFERSON STARSHIP, CARLOS SANTANA, AND THE DEAD. KSAN 95 FM BROADCAST THE DEAD'S SET LIVE — THE LAST TIME SAN FRANCISCO'S LEGENDARY ROCK STATION WOULD BROADCAST A DEAD SHOW. A SINGLE SET, ROUGHLY SEVENTY-SIX MINUTES — DRASTICALLY SHORTER THAN THE BAND'S USUAL THREE-PLUS HOURS. NO ROOM FOR THE LONG JAMS. BUT THE NOT FADE AWAY FEATURES SANTANA AND CIPOLLINA AS GUEST GUITARISTS, AND GREG ERRICO OF SLY AND THE FAMILY STONE JOINED ON DRUMS FOR THE U.S. BLUES ENCORE. FOR THE FINALE, EVERY PERFORMER FROM THE DAY GATHERED ONSTAGE FOR JOAN BAEZ LEADING "AMAZING GRACE." THE DEAD'S FIRST SHOW OF 1980. THE CAUSE WAS REAL, THE STAGE WAS CROWDED, AND THE NEW DECADE HAD BEGUN.
1980-03-30
Capitol Theatre
192w
MARCH 30, 1980 — CAPITOL THEATRE, PASSAIC, NEW JERSEY. A FORMER PORN THEATER TURNED ROCK PALACE — THIRTY-ONE HUNDRED SEATS ON A SINGLE FLOOR, JOHN SCHER RUNNING THE ROOM WITH CREW HE'D HIRED FROM THE DEFUNCT FILLMORE EAST. THE ORIGINAL ORNAMENT WAS STRIPPED AND PAINTED DULL OFF-WHITE, BUT THE ACOUSTICS WERE SUPERB. FAR FROM ME, BRENT MYDLAND'S FIRST ORIGINAL COMPOSITION, GETS ITS LIVE DEBUT TONIGHT — A QUIET MILESTONE IN THE MIDDLE OF A TWELVE-SONG FIRST SET. THE ENCORE IS WHERE THE STORY IS. JOHN BELUSHI ASKED TO SING BACKUP ON U.S. BLUES. PHIL LESH SAID NO. BELUSHI, BEING BELUSHI, DID NOT TAKE NO FOR AN ANSWER. RIGHT BEFORE THE CHORUS HE CARTWHEELED ONTO THE STAGE WEARING A SPORT COAT WITH SMALL AMERICAN FLAGS STUFFED INTO BOTH BREAST POCKETS, LANDING HIS FINAL CARTWHEEL IN PERFECT TIME TO GRAB A MICROPHONE. KREUTZMANN LATER PRAISED HIS "IMPECCABLE COMEDIC TIMING, MUSICALITY, BALLS, THE WORKS." THE BLUES BROTHERS MOVIE COMES OUT IN TWO MONTHS. BELUSHI WILL BE DEAD IN LESS THAN TWO YEARS. SATURDAY NIGHT LIVE IS NEXT WEEK. THE BAND DOESN'T KNOW THAT TOMORROW NIGHT ON THIS SAME STAGE THEY'LL DEBUT FEEL LIKE A STRANGER.

MAY 15, 1980 — NASSAU COLISEUM, LONG ISLAND. BUILT ON A FORMER MILITARY AIRFIELD, SEVEN MILES EAST OF QUEENS — FIFTEEN THOUSAND SEATS, HOME OF THE ISLANDERS. THE LAZY LIGHTNING INTO SUPPLICATION IS THE REASON TO PRESS PLAY. ONE LISTENER PUT IT SIMPLY: "I KNOW OF NO BETTER SUPPLICATION." THE BAND LOCKS INTO A PROPULSIVE GROOVE WHERE EVERY MUSICIAN IS CHASING THE SAME BEAT AND NOBODY BLINKS. THE SECOND SET IS ONE CONTINUOUS ARC — TERRAPIN STATION INTO PLAYING IN THE BAND INTO UNCLE JOHN'S BAND INTO DRUMS INTO SPACE INTO NOT FADE AWAY INTO GOIN' DOWN THE ROAD FEELING BAD INTO GOOD LOVIN'. NO STOPS. NO BREAKS. FEEL LIKE A STRANGER APPEARS IN THE FIRST SET — STILL RAW AND UNPREDICTABLE, ONLY SIX WEEKS OLD, ONE OF THE RARE DEAD SONGS THAT WAS RECORDED IN THE STUDIO BEFORE ITS LIVE DEBUT. THE SHOW WAS ORIGINALLY RECORDED FOR THE KING BISCUIT FLOWER HOUR AND BROADCAST ON FM RADIO A MONTH LATER. SELECTIONS FROM THIS NIGHT AND THE NEXT WERE LATER COMPILED AS GO TO NASSAU, THE 2002 RHINO RELEASE — ITS TITLE A WORDPLAY ON GO TO HEAVEN, RELEASED SEVENTEEN DAYS BEFORE THIS SHOW.

MAY 16, 1980 — NASSAU COLISEUM. NIGHT TWO. CHINA CAT SUNFLOWER INTO I KNOW YOU RIDER CLOSES THE FIRST SET — UNUSUAL POSITIONING FOR THE SIGNATURE PAIRING, WHICH TYPICALLY ANCHORS THE SECOND SET. WHEN THE DEAD SHUFFLED THEIR SEQUENCING LIKE THIS IT USUALLY MEANT THEY WERE FEELING LOOSE AND CONFIDENT. HIGH TIME SITS IN THE FIRST SET — A WORKINGMAN'S DEAD BALLAD THAT MOST FANS HAVE NEVER HEARD LIVE, A DEEP CUT GARCIA RESERVED FOR NIGHTS WHEN HE WAS FEELING INTROSPECTIVE. THE SECOND SET CLOSES WITH A TRUCKIN' INTO MORNING DEW SEQUENCE, AND ONE FIRST-TIME LISTENER DESCRIBED THE DEW AS "PARTICULARLY MOVING" AND "CATHARTIC" — THE KIND OF WORD NOBODY USES ABOUT A ROCK CONCERT UNLESS THEY MEAN IT. BROKEDOWN PALACE FOR THE ENCORE. ALSO RECORDED FOR THE KING BISCUIT FLOWER HOUR AND COMBINED WITH THE PREVIOUS NIGHT ON GO TO NASSAU. THE THREE BEST SONGS ON THE TAPE, ACCORDING TO LISTENERS WHO WERE THERE: EYES OF THE WORLD, MORNING DEW, BROKEDOWN PALACE. THREE GARCIA SONGS. THREE DIFFERENT EMOTIONAL REGISTERS. ALL OF THEM ON THIS TAPE.

OCTOBER 4, 1980 — WARFIELD THEATRE, SAN FRANCISCO. NIGHT TEN OF FIFTEEN. THE WARFIELD IS A G. ALBERT LANSBURGH THEATER FROM 1922 — TWENTY-THREE HUNDRED SEATS, GOLD-LEAFED OPERA BOXES, ORNATE PLASTERWORK, AND ABOVE THE PROSCENIUM AN ALBERT HERTER SUPERMURAL OF SPANISH DANCERS FLOATING IN CRIMSON AND ORANGE AGAINST DEEP BLUE. RESERVED SEATING THROUGHOUT — NOVEL FOR BAY AREA DEADHEADS USED TO GENERAL ADMISSION. THE "NO DANCING" POLICY SENT PEOPLE INTO THE HALLWAYS, WHERE BILL GRAHAM HAD PLACED SPEAKERS SO THEY COULD STILL HEAR. PHOTOGRAPHER BOB MINKIN HAD TO SLEEP ON THE STREET WITH HUNDREDS OF OTHERS JUST TO GET A PLACE IN THE TICKET LINE. THREE SETS TONIGHT: ACOUSTIC FIRST, THEN TWO ELECTRIC. THIRTY-FOUR SONGS ACROSS THREE HOURS. THE ACOUSTIC SET FEATURES SONGS FANS HAD ONLY DREAMED OF HEARING LIVE, AND BY NIGHT TEN THE ARRANGEMENTS HAVE FOUND A QUIET INTENSITY THAT THE ELECTRIC SETS CANNOT TOUCH. MATERIAL FROM THESE FIFTEEN NIGHTS BECAME THE ALBUMS RECKONING AND DEAD SET. GARCIA WOULD PLAY THIS ROOM ROUGHLY A HUNDRED TIMES ACROSS HIS CAREER.

THE SCARLET INTO FIRE IN THE THIRD SET IS THE FLAGSHIP PAIRING IN PEAK FORM. THE GRATEFUL SECONDS BLOG CALLED THIS "THE GEM OF THE 1980 THREE-SET SHOWS." THE BAND HEADS TO RADIO CITY MUSIC HALL IN ONE WEEK. OCTOBER 31, 1980 — RADIO CITY MUSIC HALL. HALLOWEEN. THE FINAL NIGHT. AL FRANKEN AND TOM DAVIS — SNL WRITERS WHO'D HELPED GET THE DEAD BOOKED ON SATURDAY NIGHT LIVE — INTRODUCED THE BAND AND HOSTED THE BROADCAST. AMONG THEIR PRE-TAPED SKETCHES: A JERRY LEWIS TELETHON PARODY FOR "JERRY'S KIDS," BITS ADMIRING BOB WEIR'S HAIR, AND A SEGMENT WHERE DAVIS "MISTAKENLY" DRANK ACID-DOSED URINE. SETTLED OUT OF COURT — THE DEAD COULD RELEASE THE SHOWTIME SPECIAL BUT WERE FORBIDDEN FROM USING RADIO CITY LOGOS ON SUBSEQUENT ALBUMS. SAGE AND SPIRIT, A WEIR INSTRUMENTAL, GETS ITS FINAL PERFORMANCE EVER TONIGHT — ONLY PLAYED LIVE TWICE. LITTLE SADIE HADN'T BEEN PLAYED SINCE FEBRUARY 1970 — A TEN-YEAR GAP. PHIL LESH'S AMP PRODUCED SPOOKY FEEDBACK AT THE START AND HE WAS TEMPORARILY SIDELINED. GARCIA AND WEIR IN THEIR WHITE GO TO HEAVEN SUITS. THE SPACE SEGMENT WAS EXTRA SPOOKY, BEFITTING THE DATE. THE FOOTAGE BECAME THE DEAD AHEAD CONCERT FILM. EIGHT NIGHTS AT RADIO CITY. A BAND THAT STARTED IN A PALO ALTO PIZZA PARLOR.
