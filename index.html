MAY 14, 1974 — ADAMS FIELD HOUSE, UNIVERSITY OF MONTANA, MISSOULA. THE ONLY GRATEFUL DEAD SHOW EVER PLAYED IN MONTANA. SEVEN THOUSAND PEOPLE AT SIX DOLLARS A HEAD. IT SNOWED. THE LOCAL PAPER CALLED THE DARK STAR AN "INCOHERENT" JAM THAT "RAMBLED ON FOR FORTY MINUTES" — IT WAS THE WEATHER REPORT SUITE > DARK STAR > CHINA DOLL SEQUENCE, AND IT'S ONE OF THE BEST THINGS THE BAND PLAYED ALL YEAR. AFTER THE SECOND SET, SOMEONE THREW A PLASTIC PITCHER FROM THE CROWD AND HIT WEIR IN THE HEAD. "THANKS A LOT," HE SAID. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 9.
