1977-02-26
FEBRUARY 26, 1977 — THE SWING AUDITORIUM IN SAN BERNARDINO. A TEN-THOUSAND-SEAT BARN BUILT IN 1949 WHERE THE ROLLING STONES MADE THEIR AMERICAN CONCERT DEBUT. TONIGHT IT HOSTS THE FIRST SHOW OF 1977 AND THE DEBUT OF TWO SONGS THAT WILL DEFINE THE YEAR: TERRAPIN STATION OPENS THE SHOW AND ESTIMATED PROPHET FOLLOWS THREE SONGS LATER. ROBERT HUNTER LATER WROTE THAT HE AND GARCIA HAD THE VISION FOR TERRAPIN INDEPENDENTLY, AT THE SAME TIME — 'THE FIRST NIGHT IT WAS PLAYED TOOK ME ABOUT AS CLOSE AS I EVER EXPECT TO GET TO FEELING CERTAIN WE WERE DOING WHAT WE WERE PUT HERE TO DO.' THE HELP ON THE WAY > SLIPKNOT! > FRANKLIN'S TOWER SUITE ALSO APPEARS FOR THE FIRST TIME AS A COMPLETE PIECE. AN EYEWITNESS IN THE FRONT ROW WATCHED STEVE PARISH PLUG GARCIA'S GUITAR INTO KEITH'S MOOG SYNTHESIZER FOR THE NEW ENVELOPE FILTER SOUND ON ESTIMATED PROPHET. THE PLAYING IN THE BAND > THE WHEEL > PLAYING IN THE BAND THAT CLOSES THE FIRST SET IS A STATEMENT OF INTENT. A SMALL PLANE CRASHED INTO THE BUILDING IN 1981 AND IT WAS RAZED. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 29.
1977-03-18
MARCH 18, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. THE HOME COURT. THREE WEEKS AFTER THE SWING AUDITORIUM OPENER, THE BAND RETURNS TO WINTERLAND FOR THREE NIGHTS AND DEBUTS FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN — NOT AS A STANDALONE SONG BUT ALREADY PAIRED WITH SCARLET BEGONIAS, THE COMBINATION THAT WILL BECOME THEIR SIGNATURE FOR THE NEXT EIGHTEEN YEARS. THIS IS THE FIRST SCARLET > FIRE EVER PLAYED. THE PAIRING WOULD APPEAR NEARLY 250 MORE TIMES THROUGH 1995. EVERYTHING ABOUT THE SPRING TOUR STARTS HERE.
1977-03-19
MARCH 19, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. SECOND OF THREE NIGHTS. THE SCARLET > FIRE FROM THE NIGHT BEFORE WAS A DEBUT — TONIGHT THE BAND IS STILL WORKING OUT WHAT THE PAIRING CAN DO. THE TERRAPIN STATION HAS SOME TAPE FLUTTER BUT THE PERFORMANCE IS STRONG. THREE NIGHTS AT WINTERLAND BEFORE THE SPRING TOUR WAS A REHEARSAL IN FRONT OF A HOME CROWD — THE BAND TESTING NEW MATERIAL WHERE THEY FELT SAFEST.
1977-04-22
APRIL 22, 1977 — THE SPECTRUM IN PHILADELPHIA. THE SPRING TOUR BEGINS IN EARNEST. THE SPECTRUM WAS AN 18,000-SEAT ARENA IN SOUTH PHILADELPHIA, HOME OF THE FLYERS AND 76ERS, SURROUNDED BY PARKING LOTS AND THE SPORTS COMPLEX. PHILLY IN 1977 WAS ROCKY COUNTRY — THE MOVIE HAD COME OUT FIVE MONTHS EARLIER AND THE ART MUSEUM STEPS WERE ALREADY A PILGRIMAGE SITE. WMMR, ONE OF THE BEST ROCK STATIONS IN THE COUNTRY, HAD BEEN BUILDING ANTICIPATION. THE BAND WOULD PLAY TWENTY-SIX DATES OVER THE NEXT FIVE WEEKS, SWEEPING FROM PHILADELPHIA THROUGH NEW ENGLAND, DOWN TO THE DEEP SOUTH, AND BACK UP THE COAST.
1977-04-23
APRIL 23, 1977 — THE SPRINGFIELD CIVIC CENTER IN SPRINGFIELD, MASSACHUSETTS. THE BIRTHPLACE OF BASKETBALL AND A BLUE-COLLAR CITY IN THE CONNECTICUT VALLEY. THE CIVIC CENTER WAS A 9,000-SEAT BRUTALIST CONCRETE BOX THAT SERVED DEADHEADS FROM WESTERN NEW ENGLAND WHO WERE TOO FAR FROM BOSTON TO MAKE THE GARDEN. SECOND DATE OF THE SPRING TOUR PROPER. ONE FAN WROTE THAT THIS SHOW 'REWARDS A SHARP FOCUS ON THE DETAILS' — THE KIND OF NIGHT WHERE NOTHING SPECTACULAR HAPPENS BUT EVERYTHING IS PLAYED WITH UNCOMMON PRECISION. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 53 (BONUS TRACKS).
1977-04-25
APRIL 25, 1977 — THE CAPITOL THEATRE IN PASSAIC, NEW JERSEY. FIRST OF THREE NIGHTS. THE CAPITOL WAS A 3,200-SEAT MOVIE PALACE FROM 1926 THAT BILL GRAHAM AND JOHN SCHER TURNED INTO THE PREMIER ROCK VENUE IN NORTHERN NEW JERSEY. ORNATE PLASTERWORK, A BALCONY, AND EXCELLENT ACOUSTICS — THE KIND OF ROOM WHERE THE DEAD SOUNDED BEST. THREE NIGHTS IN PASSAIC BEFORE FIVE AT THE PALLADIUM IN NEW YORK GAVE THE BAND EIGHT SHOWS IN TEN DAYS ACROSS THE HUDSON.
1977-04-27
APRIL 27, 1977 — THE CAPITOL THEATRE IN PASSAIC, NEW JERSEY. THIRD AND FINAL NIGHT. BY NOW THE BAND HAS BEEN IN THE NEW YORK AREA FOR THREE DAYS AND THE RAPPORT BETWEEN STAGE AND AUDIENCE IS LOCKED IN. TOMORROW NIGHT THEY CROSS THE RIVER TO THE PALLADIUM.
1977-04-29
APRIL 29, 1977 — THE PALLADIUM IN NEW YORK CITY. FIRST OF FIVE NIGHTS. THE PALLADIUM ON EAST 14TH STREET WAS A 3,000-SEAT THEATER WHERE THE RAMONES, PATTI SMITH, TELEVISION, AND THE DEAD ALL PLAYED — THE ONLY VENUE IN NEW YORK WHERE THOSE WORLDS PASSED THROUGH THE SAME DOORS ON DIFFERENT NIGHTS. NEW YORK IN 1977 WAS BROKE, DANGEROUS, AND ELECTRIC — THE SON OF SAM WAS HUNTING, STUDIO 54 HAD JUST OPENED, CBGB WAS AROUND THE CORNER ON THE BOWERY, AND THE WORLD TRADE CENTER TOWERS WERE SIX YEARS OLD AND STILL THE TALLEST BUILDINGS IN THE WORLD. FIVE NIGHTS HERE WAS A RESIDENCY, NOT A TOUR STOP.
1977-04-30
APRIL 30, 1977 — THE PALLADIUM IN NEW YORK CITY. SECOND OF FIVE NIGHTS. THE BAND IS SETTLING INTO THE RESIDENCY. OUTSIDE ON 14TH STREET, THE NEIGHBORHOOD IS A MIX OF PUNK KIDS HEADED TO THE BOWERY AND DEADHEADS WHO'D FOLLOWED THE TOUR DOWN FROM NEW ENGLAND. INSIDE, THE THEATER'S STEEP BALCONY AND TIGHT FLOOR PUT EVERYONE CLOSE TO THE STAGE.
1977-05-04
MAY 4, 1977 — THE PALLADIUM IN NEW YORK CITY. THE LAST OF THE PALLADIUM NIGHTS. FIVE SHOWS IN SEVEN DAYS AT ONE VENUE — THE BAND PLAYING WITH THE COMFORT OF A RESIDENCY AND THE ENERGY OF A CROWD THAT CAME BACK NIGHT AFTER NIGHT. THE EYES OF THE WORLD IS MONUMENTAL. TOMORROW THE TOUR HEADS NORTH TO NEW HAVEN, THEN BOSTON, THEN CORNELL. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 50.
1977-05-05
MAY 5, 1977 — THE NEW HAVEN COLISEUM IN NEW HAVEN, CONNECTICUT. A 9,000-SEAT ARENA THAT WAS THE LARGEST INDOOR VENUE BETWEEN NEW YORK AND BOSTON. YALE UNIVERSITY IS THREE BLOCKS AWAY. THE BAND LEFT THE PALLADIUM YESTERDAY AND ARRIVES HERE FOR THE FIRST OF THREE CONSECUTIVE NIGHTS IN THREE DIFFERENT CITIES — NEW HAVEN, BOSTON, CORNELL — THAT WOULD BECOME THE MOST FAMOUS STRETCH OF THE TOUR. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977 BOX SET.
1977-05-07
MAY 7, 1977 — BOSTON GARDEN. A CRAMPED, SWELTERING, BELOVED ARENA FROM 1928 SITTING DIRECTLY ABOVE NORTH STATION. NO AIR CONDITIONING. STEEL COLUMNS BLOCKING VIEWS. LOW CEILINGS THAT TRAPPED SOUND AND HEAT. THE GARDEN WAS EVERYTHING A MODERN ARENA IS NOT AND DEADHEADS LOVED IT FOR EXACTLY THAT REASON. TONIGHT: EQUIPMENT PROBLEMS PLAGUED THE FIRST SET WITH LONG BREAKS BETWEEN SONGS. THE BAND ROSE ABOVE IT. THE MISSISSIPPI HALF-STEP IS ARGUED BY MANY TO BE THE BEST VERSION EVER PLAYED. TOMORROW IS CORNELL. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977: GET SHOWN THE LIGHT BOX SET AND SEPARATELY ON VINYL FOR RECORD STORE DAY 2023.
1977-05-08
MAY 8, 1977 — BARTON HALL AT CORNELL UNIVERSITY IN ITHACA, NEW YORK. MOTHER'S DAY. THE MOST FAMOUS GRATEFUL DEAD RECORDING IN EXISTENCE. BARTON HALL IS A MILITARY DRILL HALL WITH A WOODEN FLOOR — A CAVERNOUS GYMNASIUM ON AN ISOLATED CAMPUS IN THE FINGER LAKES REGION. THE CONCERT WAS A BENEFIT — TICKETS WERE ABOUT FIVE DOLLARS. FANS DROVE FROM AS FAR AS NORTH CAROLINA. THE DAY STARTED WARM AND SUNNY. THE SCARLET BEGONIAS > FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN THAT OPENS THE SECOND SET IS CONSIDERED THE DEFINITIVE VERSION. THE MORNING DEW THAT CLOSES IT IS REGULARLY CITED AS GARCIA'S FINEST. WHEN THE AUDIENCE EMERGED FROM BARTON HALL AFTER THE SHOW, THEY WALKED INTO A MAY SNOWSTORM — BIG BILLOWING FLAKES SWIRLING IN THE PARKING LOT LIGHTS. THE TAPE BECAME THE MOST COPIED AND DISTRIBUTED CONCERT RECORDING IN HISTORY, PASSED HAND TO HAND THROUGH THE MAIL FOR DECADES BEFORE THE INTERNET EXISTED. IN 2012, THE LIBRARY OF CONGRESS ADDED IT TO THE NATIONAL RECORDING REGISTRY. WEIR, ASKED ABOUT THE SHOW YEARS LATER: 'I CAN'T REMEMBER THAT SPECIFICALLY.' RELEASED AS A STANDALONE ALBUM IN 2017 AND AS PART OF THE MAY 1977: GET SHOWN THE LIGHT BOX SET.
1977-05-09
MAY 9, 1977 — THE WAR MEMORIAL AUDITORIUM IN BUFFALO, NEW YORK. AN ART DECO BUILDING FROM 1940, BUILT AS A WPA PROJECT — THE KIND OF GRAND OLD HALL WITH HIGH CEILINGS AND ORNATE DETAILING THAT MODERN ARENAS CAN'T REPLICATE. BUFFALO WAS STILL DIGGING OUT FROM THE BLIZZARD OF '77, WHICH HAD BURIED THE CITY UNDER DRIFTS THAT REACHED SECOND-STORY WINDOWS. THE NIGHT AFTER CORNELL, AND SOME COLLECTORS ARGUE THIS SHOW IS BETTER. THE HELP ON THE WAY > SLIPKNOT! > FRANKLIN'S TOWER IS CONSIDERED BY MANY TO BE THE DEFINITIVE VERSION — TIGHTER AND MORE FEROCIOUS THAN CORNELL'S. THE BAND WAS ON A RUN, AND THEY KNEW IT. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977: GET SHOWN THE LIGHT BOX SET.
1977-05-11
MAY 11, 1977 — THE ST. PAUL CIVIC CENTER IN ST. PAUL, MINNESOTA. SIXTEEN THOUSAND SEATS IN A BRUTALIST CONCRETE ARENA. THREE NIGHTS AFTER CORNELL, THE BAND IS IN THE MIDWEST — A DIFFERENT WORLD FROM THE NORTHEAST COLLEGE TOWNS. THE CIVIC CENTER WAS TORN DOWN IN 1998. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977 BOX SET.
1977-05-13
MAY 13, 1977 — THE AUDITORIUM THEATRE IN CHICAGO. A LANDMARKED BUILDING FROM 1889 DESIGNED BY ADLER AND SULLIVAN — ONE OF THE MOST BEAUTIFUL THEATERS IN AMERICA. GOLD LEAF ARCHES, STAINED GLASS, AND ACOUSTICS DESIGNED BY AN ARCHITECT WHO LITERALLY WROTE THE BOOK ON THEATER ACOUSTICS. THE DEAD PLAYING HERE WAS LIKE PUTTING A ROCK BAND IN A CATHEDRAL. GARCIA IN AN INTERVIEW: 'WE'RE HAVING FUN AGAIN.' YOU CAN HEAR IT.
1977-05-17
MAY 17, 1977 — COLEMAN COLISEUM AT THE UNIVERSITY OF ALABAMA IN TUSCALOOSA. THE DEEP SOUTH WAS NOT REGULAR DEAD TERRITORY — ALABAMA DEADHEADS USUALLY HAD TO DRIVE TO ATLANTA. TUSCALOOSA SITS ON THE BLACK WARRIOR RIVER, SURROUNDED BY RED CLAY AND PINE FOREST. BY MID-MAY THE HUMIDITY IS RELENTLESS AND THE MAGNOLIAS ARE BLOOMING. THE COLISEUM IS A 15,000-SEAT BASKETBALL ARENA ON BEAR BRYANT'S CAMPUS. THIS SHOW 'ENTERED THE PANTHEON' ONCE THE INTERNET MADE RECORDINGS ACCESSIBLE BEYOND THE REGIONAL TRADING NETWORKS. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977 BOX SET.
1977-05-18
MAY 18, 1977 — THE FOX THEATRE IN ATLANTA, GEORGIA. FIRST OF TWO NIGHTS. THE FOX IS A 4,665-SEAT MOORISH-EGYPTIAN FANTASY MOVIE PALACE FROM 1929 — A CEILING PAINTED LIKE A NIGHT SKY WITH TWINKLING STARS, MINARETS AND ONION DOMES INSIDE. IT HAD NEARLY BEEN DEMOLISHED AND WAS SAVED BY A PRESERVATION CAMPAIGN IN 1975. THE NEIGHBORHOOD AROUND IT WAS SEEDY — ADULT BOOKSTORES AND EMPTY STOREFRONTS. ATLANTA IN 1977 WAS STILL A REGIONAL CITY, NOT THE INTERNATIONAL HUB IT WOULD BECOME. MAYNARD JACKSON WAS THREE YEARS INTO HIS TERM AS THE FIRST BLACK MAYOR OF A MAJOR SOUTHERN CITY.
1977-05-19
MAY 19, 1977 — THE FOX THEATRE IN ATLANTA. SECOND NIGHT. LYNYRD SKYNYRD, THE ALLMAN BROTHERS, AND MARSHALL TUCKER WERE THE LOCAL HEROES — THE DEAD WERE VISITORS IN SOUTHERN ROCK COUNTRY. THE FOX'S ACOUSTICS AND INTIMACY (4,665 SEATS) SUITED THE BAND BETTER THAN THE ARENAS THEY'D BEEN PLAYING ALL MONTH. FIVE MONTHS FROM NOW, A PLANE CRASH IN MISSISSIPPI WOULD KILL RONNIE VAN ZANT AND END SKYNYRD. BUT TONIGHT THE SOUTH IS STILL SKYNYRD'S. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 29.
1977-05-21
MAY 21, 1977 — THE LAKELAND CIVIC CENTER IN LAKELAND, FLORIDA. A FUNCTIONAL CONCRETE BOX BETWEEN TAMPA AND ORLANDO — AIR-CONDITIONED, WHICH MATTERED IN MAY IN CENTRAL FLORIDA. THE DETROIT TIGERS HELD SPRING TRAINING IN LAKELAND, BUT BY LATE MAY THE SNOWBIRDS AND BASEBALL PEOPLE WERE GONE AND THE TOWN WAS QUIET. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 29 (BONUS TRACKS).
1977-05-22
MAY 22, 1977 — THE SPORTATORIUM IN PEMBROKE PINES, FLORIDA. A CONVERTED JAI ALAI FRONTON WITH MINIMAL AIR CONDITIONING IN SOUTH FLORIDA — A BARE-BONES, SWEATY, NO-FRILLS ROCK VENUE WHERE BANDS AND AUDIENCES WERE CRAMMED TOGETHER. DICK LATVALA, THE BAND'S ORIGINAL ARCHIVIST, CONSIDERED THIS THE FINEST SHOW OF THE ENTIRE SPRING TOUR — ABOVE CORNELL, ABOVE BUFFALO, ABOVE EVERYTHING. THE WHARF RAT > TERRAPIN STATION > MORNING DEW SEQUENCE IN THE SECOND SET IS THE ONLY TIME THE BAND EVER STARTED TERRAPIN FROM THE 'INSPIRATION' VERSE. THAT ALONE MAKES THIS TAPE ESSENTIAL. RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 3.
1977-05-25
MAY 25, 1977 — THE MOSQUE IN RICHMOND, VIRGINIA. STAR WARS OPENED TODAY IN THEATERS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. THE MOSQUE IS A 3,600-SEAT SHRINERS TEMPLE FROM 1927 WITH MOORISH-INSPIRED ARCHITECTURE — HORSESHOE ARCHES AND ORNAMENTAL TILE. RICHMOND WAS A TOBACCO CITY, HUMID AND TRADITIONAL. THIS IS THE VERY FIRST DAVE'S PICKS RELEASE — VOLUME 1 — WHICH MAKES IT THE SHOW THE BAND'S ARCHIVIST CHOSE TO LAUNCH THE ENTIRE SERIES. DAVID LEMIEUX: 'IT'S AS CLOSE TO A FLAWLESS GRATEFUL DEAD TOUR AS I'VE EVER HEARD.' RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 1.
1977-05-26
MAY 26, 1977 — THE BALTIMORE CIVIC CENTER. STAR WARS HAD BEEN IN THEATERS FOR ONE DAY. THE CIVIC CENTER WAS A 12,000-SEAT ARENA DOWNTOWN, HOME OF THE BULLETS AND THE CIVIC PRIDE OF A BLUE-COLLAR PORT CITY. THE SPRING TOUR IS WINDING DOWN — AFTER TONIGHT, ONLY HARTFORD REMAINS. THE BAND HAS PLAYED TWENTY-FOUR SHOWS IN FIVE WEEKS. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 41.
1977-06-08
JUNE 8, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. FIRST OF TWO NIGHTS. THE SPRING TOUR IS OVER AND THE BAND IS HOME. THEY'VE HAD TEN DAYS OFF AFTER HARTFORD. THE PORTLAND TRAIL BLAZERS WON THE NBA CHAMPIONSHIP THREE DAYS AGO — BILL WALTON, A KNOWN DEADHEAD, LED THEM TO THE TITLE. SAN FRANCISCO WAS IN A GOOD MOOD. THESE WINTERLAND SHOWS ARE THE SPRING TOUR'S VICTORY LAP.
1977-06-09
JUNE 9, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. THE SEASON FINALE. AFTER TONIGHT THE BAND RETREATS TO RECORD TERRAPIN STATION AND WON'T PLAY AGAIN UNTIL SEPTEMBER. ONE FAN DESCRIBED THE JUNE WINTERLAND SHOWS AS 'THE CULMINATION OF THE PREVIOUS MONTH — THE BAND RETURNING HOME TO SHOW ITS FANS WHAT'S BEEN GOING DOWN.' THEY PLAYED SIX MONTHS OF 1977 IN LESS THAN SIXTY SHOWS AND PRODUCED WHAT IS WIDELY CONSIDERED THE GREATEST SUSTAINED RUN OF LIVE MUSIC IN ROCK HISTORY.
1977-09-03
SEPTEMBER 3, 1977 — RACEWAY PARK IN ENGLISHTOWN, NEW JERSEY. LABOR DAY WEEKEND. THE LARGEST TICKETED SINGLE-ACT CONCERT IN AMERICAN HISTORY AT THE TIME — OVER 107,000 TICKETS SOLD, WITH ACTUAL ATTENDANCE ESTIMATED BETWEEN 125,000 AND 175,000. THE FIRST MILLION-DOLLAR GATE IN THE BAND'S HISTORY. MARSHALL TUCKER BAND AND NEW RIDERS OF THE PURPLE SAGE OPENED. A WOMAN WENT INTO LABOR DURING MARSHALL TUCKER'S SET AND WAS EVACUATED BY HELICOPTER — SHE GAVE BIRTH TO A HEALTHY BOY. MANALAPAN TOWNSHIP HAD SUED TO STOP THE CONCERT, AND THE BAND HAD TO PROVE THE HELLS ANGELS WERE NOT PROVIDING SECURITY. THE CONCESSIONS OPERATOR REPORTED SELLING 200,000 SODA CANS AND 15,000 POUNDS OF HOT DOGS. IT WAS ALSO THE BAND'S FIRST SHOW IN THREE MONTHS — MICKEY HART HAD BEEN RECOVERING FROM A CAR ACCIDENT. FIRST TRUCKIN' IN OVER TWO YEARS. PHIL LESH: 'LET'S HEAR IT FOR YOU, 'CAUSE THIS IS THE BEST CROWD WE'VE SEEN IN A LOOONG TIME.' RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 15.
1977-09-28
SEPTEMBER 28, 1977 — THE PARAMOUNT THEATRE IN SEATTLE, WASHINGTON. THE FALL TOUR BEGINS ON THE WEST COAST. THREE MONTHS OFF WHILE MICKEY HART RECOVERED FROM A CAR ACCIDENT AND THE TERRAPIN STATION ALBUM WAS RELEASED TO MIXED REVIEWS — THE BAND THEMSELVES WERE UNHAPPY WITH THE PRODUCTION. BUT THE LIVE SHOW IS THE PRODUCT. THE ALBUM IS THE SOUVENIR. THE PARAMOUNT IS AN ELEGANT THEATER, AND SEATTLE IN OCTOBER IS THE START OF THE LONG RAIN.
1977-10-01
OCTOBER 1, 1977 — THE PARAMOUNT THEATRE IN PORTLAND, OREGON. FIRST OF TWO NIGHTS. PORTLAND IN 1977 WAS SMALL, AFFORDABLE, AND HAPPILY FORGOTTEN BY THE REST OF AMERICA — TIMBER INDUSTRY, POWELL'S BOOKS, AND A LEFT-OF-CENTER VIBE THAT MADE IT NATURAL DEAD TERRITORY. BILL WALTON HAD LED THE BLAZERS TO THE CHAMPIONSHIP FOUR MONTHS EARLIER AND THE CITY WAS STILL FLOATING. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 45.
1977-10-02
OCTOBER 2, 1977 — THE PARAMOUNT THEATRE IN PORTLAND. SECOND NIGHT. THE FIRST CASEY JONES SINCE 1974. THE FIRST DUPREE'S DIAMOND BLUES IN EIGHT YEARS. TWO BREAKOUTS IN ONE SHOW — THE BAND WAS DIGGING INTO ITS CATALOG AND FINDING SONGS IT HADN'T TOUCHED IN YEARS. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 45.
1977-10-07
OCTOBER 7, 1977 — THE UNIVERSITY ARENA IN ALBUQUERQUE, NEW MEXICO. KNOWN AS 'THE PIT' — A 15,000-SEAT ARENA BUILT BELOW GROUND LEVEL SO FANS ENTER AT THE TOP AND WALK DOWN TO THEIR SEATS. OUTSIDE, THE COTTONWOOD TREES ALONG THE RIO GRANDE WERE TURNING GOLD AND THE SANDIA MOUNTAINS CAUGHT THE SUNSET IN WATERMELON PINK. THE AIR AT FIVE THOUSAND FEET IS THIN AND DRY, CARRYING THE SMELL OF PINON AND SAGE. THE SOUTHWEST SWING: AFTER PORTLAND AND SEATTLE, THE TOUR DROPPED SOUTH THROUGH DESERT COUNTRY. FEATURED ON ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 1, NUMBER 2.
1977-10-09
OCTOBER 9, 1977 — MCNICHOLS SPORTS ARENA IN DENVER, COLORADO. ALTITUDE: 5,280 FEET. THE BAND'S FIRST VISIT TO MCNICHOLS, AN 18,000-SEAT ARENA WITH A DISTINCTIVE WHITE EXTERIOR THAT OPENED JUST TWO YEARS EARLIER. DENVER FANS WERE DEVOTED — THE DEAD WOULD PLAY HERE MORE THAN TWENTY TIMES. DURING SET BREAK, FANS WATCHED THE MUHAMMAD ALI FIGHT ON A SMALL TELEVISION.
1977-10-11
OCTOBER 11, 1977 — THE LLOYD NOBLE CENTER AT THE UNIVERSITY OF OKLAHOMA IN NORMAN. FANS REPORTED A STRANGELY LIFELESS CROWD — SITTING IN THEIR SEATS, NOT RETURNING THE BAND'S ENERGY. THERE WAS NO ENCORE. BUT THE MUSIC TOLD A DIFFERENT STORY: THE HELP ON THE WAY > SLIPKNOT! > FRANKLIN'S TOWER, THE PEGGY-O, AND THE LET IT GROW ARE ALL CALLED OUT AS HIGHLIGHTS OF THE FALL TOUR. SOMETIMES THE BAND PLAYED HARDEST WHEN THE ROOM WASN'T GIVING ANYTHING BACK. RELEASED AS BONUS TRACKS ON DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 29 AND FEATURED ON ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 1, NUMBER 2.
1977-10-28
OCTOBER 28, 1977 — SOLDIERS AND SAILORS MEMORIAL HALL IN KANSAS CITY, KANSAS. A 3,500-SEAT VETERANS MEMORIAL FROM 1924 WITH NEOCLASSICAL COLUMNS AND HONOR ROLLS ON THE WALLS. DEADHEADS TWIRLING IN THE AISLES OF A BUILDING DESIGNED FOR GRAVITAS. THE YANKEES HAD BEATEN THE DODGERS IN THE WORLD SERIES TEN DAYS EARLIER — REGGIE JACKSON'S THREE-HOME-RUN GAME WAS STILL THE TALK OF THE COUNTRY. THE CASEY JONES ENCORE WAS DESCRIBED AS THE LONGEST AND MOST FEROCIOUS VERSION OF THE SONG ANYONE HAD HEARD.
1977-10-29
OCTOBER 29, 1977 — EVANS FIELD HOUSE AT NORTHERN ILLINOIS UNIVERSITY IN DEKALB, ILLINOIS. A COLLEGE FIELD HOUSE IN A SMALL MIDWESTERN TOWN SURROUNDED BY CORN COUNTRY — HARVEST WAS JUST FINISHING, THE STUBBLE FIELDS STRETCHING TO THE HORIZON, GRAIN DRYERS HUMMING. WIDELY CONSIDERED ONE OF THE BEST SHOWS FROM THE SECOND HALF OF 1977. THE ESTIMATED PROPHET DISSOLVES INTO A JAZZY ENDING THAT TRANSFORMS INTO AN EYES OF THE WORLD REGARDED AS AMONG THE FINEST. THE BAND WAS DESCRIBED AS 'A LIMBER ENSEMBLE THAT REFUSED TO WASTE A NOTE.' RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 33.
1977-10-30
OCTOBER 30, 1977 — ASSEMBLY HALL AT INDIANA UNIVERSITY IN BLOOMINGTON. A 17,000-SEAT CONCRETE DOME THAT LOOKS LIKE A FLYING SAUCER FROM THE OUTSIDE — PRE-STRESSED CONCRETE SHELL SUPPORTED BY TWO INTERLOCKING ARCHES, NO INTERIOR COLUMNS. BOB KNIGHT'S BASKETBALL PALACE. THIS WAS THE ONLY GRATEFUL DEAD SHOW EVER PLAYED IN BLOOMINGTON. THE PLAYING IN THE BAND > THE OTHER ONE > DRUMS > THE WHEEL > WHARF RAT > PLAYING IN THE BAND SECOND SET CLOSES WITH A FULL-TILT JOHNNY B. GOODE.
1977-11-01
NOVEMBER 1, 1977 — COBO ARENA IN DETROIT, MICHIGAN. AN 11,500-SEAT ARENA ON THE DETROIT RIVER WATERFRONT, PART OF THE CONVENTION CENTER WHERE THE ANNUAL AUTO SHOW LIVED. DETROIT IN 1977 WAS STILL A MANUFACTURING CITY — BETHLEHEM STEEL, THE BIG THREE AUTOMAKERS, A BLUE-COLLAR TOWN WITH PASSIONATE FANS. COBO WAS WHERE KISS RECORDED PARTS OF ALIVE! AND WHERE BOB SEGER WAS A HOMETOWN HERO. THE DEAD PLAYED HERE THROUGHOUT THE SEVENTIES.
1977-11-04
NOVEMBER 4, 1977 — COTTERELL GYM AT COLGATE UNIVERSITY IN HAMILTON, NEW YORK. POPULATION: THREE THOUSAND. A TINY LIBERAL ARTS COLLEGE IN RURAL CENTRAL NEW YORK. ABOUT 1,500 PEOPLE IN A COLLEGE GYMNASIUM — LOW STAGE, GENERAL ADMISSION, CLOSE ENOUGH TO SEE GARCIA'S FINGERS ON THE FRETBOARD. 'THE HEADS WERE FAR OUTNUMBERED BY THE STUDENT PREPPIES AT THEIR HOMECOMING,' ONE ATTENDEE RECALLED. PHIL INTRODUCED THE BAND AS 'THE JONES GANG.' THE FIRST-EVER EYES OF THE WORLD > ESTIMATED PROPHET SEQUENCE — A PAIRING THAT WOULDN'T BE REPEATED FOR TWO YEARS. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 12.
1977-11-05
NOVEMBER 5, 1977 — COMMUNITY WAR MEMORIAL AUDITORIUM IN ROCHESTER, NEW YORK. A ROWDY CROWD HAD BEGUN GATHERING AT THE ARENA SEVERAL DAYS BEFORE. THE BAND RESPONDED WITH WHAT WAS DESCRIBED AS 'A GEM OF A CONCERT' — HARD-DRIVING MINGLEWOOD BLUES AND DEAL, SPRIGHTLY BIG RIVER AND DIRE WOLF, SOFTER EXPLORATIONS ON CANDYMAN AND IT MUST HAVE BEEN THE ROSES. THE REEL-TO-REEL MASTERS DETERIORATED TOWARD THE END OF THE SHOW AND A CASSETTE MASTER HAD TO BE USED TO FILL IN THE GAPS. RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 34.
1977-11-06
NOVEMBER 6, 1977 — BROOME COUNTY VETERANS MEMORIAL ARENA IN BINGHAMTON, NEW YORK. THE FALL TOUR CLOSER. A COMPACT CIVIC ARENA IN THE SOUTHERN TIER — ABOUT 7,000 SEATS, SMALL ENOUGH TO FEEL INTIMATE. THREE DAYS EARLIER THE BAND HAD PLAYED A 1,500-SEAT COLLEGE GYM AT COLGATE. TWO DAYS EARLIER, A ROWDY CROWD IN ROCHESTER. TONIGHT THEY CLOSE THE RUN WITH A DUPREE'S DIAMOND BLUES — A RARITY BY 1977 — AND A MISSISSIPPI HALF-STEP THAT OPENS WITH QUIET CONFIDENCE. 1977-12-27
DECEMBER 27, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. FIRST NIGHT OF THE YEAR-END RUN. SIX WEEKS SINCE BINGHAMTON. CLOSE ENCOUNTERS OF THE THIRD KIND AND SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER ARE BOTH IN THEATERS. DEBBY BOONE'S 'YOU LIGHT UP MY LIFE' HAS BEEN NUMBER ONE FOR TEN WEEKS AND WILL NOT LEAVE. OUTSIDE, THE SHORT DECEMBER DAYS END BEFORE FIVE O'CLOCK AND FOG ROLLS THROUGH THE FILLMORE DISTRICT. THE DEAD ARE BACK AT WINTERLAND — AN OLD ICE RINK AT POST AND STEINER WHERE THEIR SKULL-AND-ROSES BANNER HANGS PERMANENTLY IN THE RAFTERS — FOR FOUR NIGHTS TO CLOSE OUT WHAT THE BAND'S ARCHIVIST WOULD LATER CALL THE GREATEST YEAR IN THEIR HISTORY.
1977-12-29
DECEMBER 29, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. THE THIRD NIGHT OF THE YEAR-END RUN, AND ONE OF THE MOST CELEBRATED DEAD SHOWS EVER PLAYED. THE HIGHLIGHT: THE RETURN OF CHINA CAT SUNFLOWER > I KNOW YOU RIDER AFTER A THREE-YEAR ABSENCE — NOT PLAYED SINCE OCTOBER 1974. THE AUDIENCE ERUPTION WHEN THE OPENING NOTES HIT IS AUDIBLE ON THE TAPE. A PHOTOGRAPHER WHO HAD TRAVELED SOLO FROM NEW YORK ON CHRISTMAS DAY VIA AMTRAK AND GREYHOUND LOST HIS WALLET AND ALL HIS TICKETS AT THE GREYHOUND TERMINAL. A WINTERLAND PRODUCTIONS EMPLOYEE NAMED GLORIA PULIDO REPLACED THEM. HE DESCRIBED WINTERLAND AS 'THE LAST OF SAN FRANCISCO'S LEGENDARY VENUES' FOR A YOUNG DEADHEAD WHO NEVER EXPERIENCED THE FILLMORE. THE CHINA CAT RETURN WAS A ONE-OFF — IT WOULDN'T APPEAR AGAIN UNTIL FEBRUARY 1979. RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 10.
1977-12-30
DECEMBER 30, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA IN SAN FRANCISCO. THE NIGHT AFTER THE CHINA CAT RETURN. CLOSE ENCOUNTERS OF THE THIRD KIND HAD OPENED IN THEATERS TWO WEEKS EARLIER. SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER WAS FOURTEEN DAYS OLD. DISCO WAS ABOUT TO SWALLOW AMERICAN MUSIC, AND HERE WAS THE GRATEFUL DEAD IN A CONVERTED ICE RINK ON POST STREET, PLAYING A MISSISSIPPI HALF-STEP AND A SHIP OF FOOLS AND AN EYES OF THE WORLD THAT SUGGESTED THEY HAD NO INTEREST IN WHAT THE REST OF THE CULTURE WAS DOING. THE ESTIMATED PROPHET > EYES OF THE WORLD > SAINT STEPHEN > SUGAR MAGNOLIA SECOND SET HAS WHAT ONE CRITIC CALLED 'A MYSTERIOUS GRACE ALL ITS OWN.' TOMORROW NIGHT IS NEW YEAR'S EVE.
