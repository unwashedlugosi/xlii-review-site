1977-02-26
THE SWING AUDITORIUM IN SAN BERNARDINO
337w
FEBRUARY 26, 1977 — THE SWING AUDITORIUM IN SAN BERNARDINO. A 10,000-SEAT ARENA BUILT IN 1949 ON THE NATIONAL ORANGE SHOW FAIRGROUNDS — NAMED AFTER THE REGION'S CITRUS INDUSTRY, THOUGH BY 1977 THE ORANGE GROVES WERE GIVING WAY TO SUBDIVISIONS AND THE BUILDING WAS CONSISTENTLY DESCRIBED AS A DUMP. FLAT FLOOR WHERE YOU COULDN'T SEE IF ANYONE STOOD UP. NO AIR CONDITIONING IN THE INLAND EMPIRE. A STAGEHAND NAMED STUMPY MANAGED THE STAGE. THE ROLLING STONES HAD MADE THEIR AMERICAN CONCERT DEBUT HERE IN 1964. SEVEN DOLLARS AND FIFTY CENTS, GENERAL ADMISSION. AN EYEWITNESS IN THE FRONT ROW RECALLED MORE HELLS ANGELS THAN HE'D SEEN AT ANY OTHER DEAD SHOW — "BECAUSE IT WAS SAN BERNARDINO." FLEETWOOD MAC'S RUMOURS HAD BEEN IN RECORD STORES FOR THREE WEEKS AND WAS ALREADY INESCAPABLE. TONIGHT IS THE FIRST SHOW OF 1977, AND THE BAND OPENS WITH THE LIVE DEBUT OF TERRAPIN STATION — A SONG THEY'VE BEEN WORKING ON IN THE STUDIO WITH PRODUCER KEITH OLSEN BUT HAVE NEVER PLAYED FOR AN AUDIENCE. IT ARRIVES FULLY FORMED. ESTIMATED PROPHET DEBUTS THREE SONGS LATER — BOB WEIR AND JOHN BARLOW'S REGGAE-INFLECTED COMPOSITION IN AN UNUSUAL 7/4 TIME SIGNATURE. ROBERT HUNTER, IN A LETTER TO GARCIA WRITTEN AFTER GARCIA'S DEATH: 'SEEMS LIKE YOU'D HAD THE VISION OF THE MUSIC ABOUT THE SAME TIME I HAD THE VISION OF THE WORDS, INDEPENDENTLY. TERRAPIN. THE FIRST NIGHT IT WAS PLAYED TOOK ME ABOUT AS CLOSE AS I EVER EXPECT TO GET TO FEELING CERTAIN WE WERE DOING WHAT WE WERE PUT HERE TO DO.' IN THE FRONT ROW, AN ATTENDEE WATCHED STEVE PARISH PLUG GARCIA'S GUITAR INTO KEITH'S MOOG SYNTHESIZER — THE FIRST USE OF THE MU-TRON ENVELOPE FILTER THAT WOULD DEFINE THE WATERY, SHIMMERING 1977 GUITAR TONE. PHIL LESH PLAYED A DISTINCT BASS SOLO DURING EYES OF THE WORLD — ONE FAN SAID IT WAS THE LAST TIME HE EVER SAW PHIL DO THAT. A TWIN-ENGINE CESSNA CRASHED INTO THE BUILDING IN 1981 AND IT WAS RAZED. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 29.
1977-03-18
WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO
276w
MARCH 18, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO. THE HOME COURT — A CONVERTED ICE RINK AT POST AND STEINER IN THE FILLMORE DISTRICT WHERE THE DEAD PLAYED MORE SHOWS THAN ANYWHERE ELSE ON EARTH. TEMPORARY PLYWOOD FLOORING COVERED THE ICE SURFACE AND YOU COULD LITERALLY BOUNCE ON IT. THE CEILING WAS MOTTLED WITH CRACKING PLASTER AND DIRTY WINDOWS. BOB WEIR CALLED IT 'AN ACOUSTICAL SNAKEPIT.' ONE REGULAR DESCRIBED IT AS 'A DUMP — AH, BUT WHAT A DUMP. IT SMELLED, AND HAD A SOUND OF ITS OWN.' THREE WEEKS AFTER THE SWING AUDITORIUM DEBUT, THE BAND IS BACK FOR THREE NIGHTS. TONIGHT THEY DEBUT FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN — NOT AS A STANDALONE SONG BUT ALREADY PAIRED WITH SCARLET BEGONIAS, THE COMBINATION THAT WILL BECOME THEIR SIGNATURE FOR THE NEXT EIGHTEEN YEARS. FIRE EMERGED 'HESITANTLY, UNEXPECTEDLY, OUT OF SCARLET' — AN ELEVEN-MINUTE FIRST ATTEMPT. THE TWO SONGS WOULD BE PAIRED APPROXIMATELY 254 MORE TIMES THROUGH JULY 1995. FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN NEVER KNEW LIFE WITHOUT ITS PARTNER. A COLLECTOR WITH THIRTY-SIX YEARS OF LISTENING CALLED THIS HIS PERSONAL FAVORITE SUGAREE AND PEGGY-O AFTER FOUR MONTHS OF COMPARING EVERY VERSION HE OWNED. PHIL LESH WAS 'PLAYING LEAD BASS ABOUT NINETY PERCENT OF THE TIME' — FAR MORE ACTIVE THAN USUAL. ONE LONGTIME LISTENER: 'NOTE THE WAY THEY REPEATEDLY MOVE FROM PLAYING LOUD TO PLAYING ULTRA QUIET WITHIN THE SAME SONG. JERRY EXPERIMENTS WITH NEW APPROACHES IN VIRTUALLY EVERY SONG.' THE NOT FADE AWAY BUILDS THROUGH MULTIPLE CHAPTERS — FROM A WAY-BACK GROOVE TO A VIOLA LEE-LIKE CRESCENDO TO A BEAUTIFULLY QUIET ENDING THAT REVEALS THE FIRST NOTES OF SAINT STEPHEN 'LIKE A SUNRISE ON THE BEACH.'
1977-03-19
WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO
233w
MARCH 19, 1977 — WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO. NIGHT TWO OF THREE. THE SCARLET > FIRE DEBUT FROM LAST NIGHT WAS STILL RESONATING INSIDE THE OLD ICE RINK, BUT TONIGHT THE BAND HAS OTHER IDEAS. THEY PLAY THE ONLY KNOWN EXTENDED VERSION OF TERRAPIN STATION — BEYOND THE USUAL LADY WITH A FAN AND TERRAPIN SECTIONS THAT BECAME THE STANDARD LIVE ARRANGEMENT, THEY VENTURE INTO THE TERRAPIN TRANSIT AND AT A SIDING MOVEMENTS FROM THE STUDIO ALBUM. ONE COLLECTOR WHO'D FOLLOWED THE BAND FOR DECADES CALLED IT 'THE ALHAMBRA' AND WROTE: 'IT'S AMAZING THAT I CAN KNOW SO MUCH DEAD MUSIC AND STILL FIND GEMS LIKE THIS THAT I DIDN'T KNOW EXISTED.' THIS WAS THE ONLY CONCERT WHERE THE BAND PLAYED MORE THAN THE STANDARD TERRAPIN ARRANGEMENT — THEY NEVER ATTEMPTED IT AGAIN. THE SECOND SET IS ONE UNBROKEN PIECE: EYES OF THE WORLD DISSOLVING INTO DANCING IN THE STREET, WHARF RAT, FRANKLIN'S TOWER, AND SUGAR MAGNOLIA WITHOUT A SEAM BETWEEN THEM. ONE FAN WHO DISCOVERED THE SHOW AFTER YEARS OF ASSUMING ALL THE 1977 TREASURES WERE IN MAY WROTE: 'ONCE I HEARD THIS SHOW I FORGOT ABOUT 3/18 AND ITS FIRST-EVER SCARLET > FIRE ALMOST ENTIRELY.' ANOTHER: 'THE EAST COAST TOUR GETS ALL THE ATTENTION, BUT DON'T MISS OUT ON THE SMOKIN' HOT WINTERLAND SHOWS FROM THIS YEAR.' THE BIG RIVER IS A BARN BURNER. THE THEY LOVE EACH OTHER IS FLAWLESS.
1977-04-22
THE SPECTRUM IN PHILADELPHIA
292w
APRIL 22, 1977 — THE SPECTRUM IN PHILADELPHIA. THE HOUSE THAT ROCK BUILT — AN 18,000-SEAT ARENA IN SOUTH PHILADELPHIA WHERE THE DEAD PLAYED MORE SHOWS THAN ANY OTHER ACT, SPRINGSTEEN INCLUDED. THE NAME WAS A TONGUE-IN-CHEEK ACRONYM: SP FOR SPORTS AND SOUTH PHILLY, E FOR ENTERTAINMENT, C FOR CIRCUSES, T FOR THEATRICALS, R FOR RECREATION, UM FOR 'UM, WHAT A NICE BUILDING!' THE BUILDER RAN OUT OF MONEY DURING CONSTRUCTION AND GOT A CODE VARIANCE ON THE ROOF MATERIAL. IT LEAKED FROM DAY ONE. FIVE MONTHS AFTER OPENING, WIND BLEW PART OF THE ROOF OFF DURING AN ICE CAPADES PERFORMANCE. TWO WEEKS LATER IT BLEW OFF AGAIN. JOHNNY CARSON MADE JOKES ON THE TONIGHT SHOW. THE SPECTRUM WAS TECHNICALLY BANKRUPT UNTIL 1971. NONE OF THIS MATTERED ONCE THE MUSIC STARTED. TONIGHT IS THE OPENING NIGHT OF THE SPRING 1977 NORTHEAST RUN — TWENTY-SIX DATES OVER FIVE WEEKS. ANNIE HALL HAD JUST OPENED IN NEW YORK TWO DAYS EARLIER. PHILLY IN 1977 WAS ROCKY COUNTRY — THE MOVIE WAS FIVE MONTHS OLD AND THE ART MUSEUM STEPS WERE ALREADY A PILGRIMAGE SITE. WMMR WAS ONE OF THE BEST FM ROCK STATIONS IN THE COUNTRY — YOU'D HEAR A TWELVE-MINUTE ALLMAN BROTHERS TRACK FOLLOWED BY STEELY DAN FOLLOWED BY A DEEP ZEPPELIN CUT. CHEESESTEAKS WERE A DOLLAR FIFTY. AN ATTENDEE RECALLED THAT 'THE CROWD IS SO INTO IT AND WILD AND CRAZY I FELT LIKE I WAS RIGHT THERE. THE YELLING, THE SCREAMING. THE CROWD SINGS HALF-STEP FOR JERRY.' ANOTHER CALLED IT 'FEISTY, OFTEN AGGRESSIVE — A BAND DIGGING THEIR TEETH INTO THE JAMS.' ONE MEMORABLE ANOMALY: THE DANCING IN THE STREETS TAKES A DETOUR INTO I GOT MY MOJO WORKIN' — A ONE-TIME-ONLY MOVE THAT NEVER HAPPENED BEFORE OR AFTER THIS NIGHT.
1977-04-23
THE SPRINGFIELD CIVIC CENTER IN SPRINGFIELD, MASSACHUSETTS
200w
APRIL 23, 1977 — THE SPRINGFIELD CIVIC CENTER IN SPRINGFIELD, MASSACHUSETTS. THE BIRTHPLACE OF BASKETBALL — JAMES NAISMITH INVENTED THE GAME HERE IN 1891 AND THE BASKETBALL HALL OF FAME IS DOWN THE ROAD. THE CIVIC CENTER WAS A BRUTALIST CONCRETE BOX FROM 1972, ABOUT 9,000 SEATS, HOME TO AHL HOCKEY AND THE PRIDE OF A BLUE-COLLAR CITY IN THE CONNECTICUT VALLEY. WESTERN MASSACHUSETTS DEADHEADS DEPENDED ON SPRINGFIELD — BOSTON WAS TOO FAR FOR A WEEKNIGHT DRIVE. AN ATTENDEE WHO WAS AT THIS SHOW, HIS SECOND NIGHT OF THE TOUR, CALLED IT HIS FAVORITE OF THE SEVENTY SHOWS HE SAW BETWEEN 1973 AND 1995: 'GENERAL ADMISSION, ON A RAINY COOL DAY. CIVIC CENTER WAS WAY SMALLER THAN MOST OF THE OTHER NORTHEASTERN HOCKEY RINK VENUES, SO IT ALWAYS FELT MORE INTIMATE AND WILD.' ONE REVIEWER NOTED: 'THAT'S JUST THEM LIGHTING THE CANDLE FOR A LIFTOFF TO WORLDS UNCHARTED!' THE SECOND SET BUILDS TO AN EXPLOSION — 'SOMEWHERE INSIDE OF FRANKLIN'S TOWER THEY UNLEASH SOME KIND OF PERFECT RHYTHM THAT JUST EXPLODES.' IT WAS A RAINY APRIL NIGHT IN A CITY THAT EXISTED ON BASKETBALL AND INSURANCE COMPANIES AND THE DEAD WERE THE MOST INTERESTING THING TO HAPPEN THERE ALL SPRING.
1977-04-25
THE CAPITOL THEATRE IN PASSAIC, NEW JERSEY
229w
APRIL 25, 1977 — THE CAPITOL THEATRE IN PASSAIC, NEW JERSEY. FIRST OF THREE NIGHTS. THE CAPITOL WAS A 3,140-SEAT MOVIE PALACE FROM 1926 THAT HAD DEGRADED INTO A PORNOGRAPHY THEATER BY THE EARLY SEVENTIES. YOUNG PROMOTER JOHN SCHER NEGOTIATED AN ARRANGEMENT: KUNG FU MOVIES AND ADULT FILMS BY DAY, ROCK CONCERTS BY NIGHT — HE PAID THE PORN DISTRIBUTOR A HUNDRED DOLLARS PER SHOW ABOVE STANDARD REVENUE. HE HIRED THE CREW FROM THE RECENTLY CLOSED FILLMORE EAST AND INSTALLED AN IN-HOUSE VIDEO SYSTEM THAT PRODUCED HIGH-QUALITY BLACK-AND-WHITE BOOTLEG FOOTAGE FOR YEARS. THE ORIGINAL ORNATE PLASTERWORK WAS STRIPPED AND PAINTED DULL OFF-WHITE, BUT THE ACOUSTICS WERE CALLED 'SUPER' — A SINGLE-FLOOR STADIUM ARRANGEMENT RISING FROM STAGE TO BACK WALL. TWO SHOWS A NIGHT ON WEEKENDS MEANT SIX THOUSAND PEOPLE THROUGH AN INTIMATE ROOM IN A SINGLE WEEKEND. THE ROLLING STONES, SPRINGSTEEN, AND THE WHO ALL PLAYED HERE. AN ATTENDEE WHO'D THUMBED DOWN FROM WESTERN MASS AND SPENT A FEW DAYS RESTING IN THE CITY BEFORE HEADING TO JERSEY DESCRIBED THE FIRST SET AS 'SUBDUED, BUT WITH EIGHT NIGHTS COMING UP I EXPECTED A SLOW BURN.' THE SCARLET > FIRE IS ONLY THE FOURTH TIME THE SONGS HAVE BEEN PAIRED — THE TRANSITION 'VERY DIFFERENT FROM WHAT IT WOULD BECOME EVEN A COUPLE OF WEEKS LATER.' THE BUILDING WAS DEMOLISHED IN APRIL 1991. A SHOPPING CENTER STANDS ON THE SITE.
1977-04-27
THE CAPITOL THEATRE, PASSAIC
188w
APRIL 27, 1977 — THE CAPITOL THEATRE, PASSAIC. THIRD AND FINAL NIGHT. THE BAND HAS BEEN IN THE NEW YORK AREA FOR THREE DAYS, PLAYING THE SAME INTIMATE 3,140-SEAT ROOM WHERE JOHN SCHER'S FORMER-FILLMORE-EAST CREW RAN A PROFESSIONAL OPERATION BEHIND A SHABBY FACADE. THERE IS IN-HOUSE VIDEO FROM THIS SHOW — BLACK-AND-WHITE FOOTAGE OF A SPRING 1977 SCARLET > FIRE, ONE OF ONLY A HANDFUL OF VIDEO DOCUMENTS FROM THE ENTIRE TOUR. TONIGHT'S OTHER CENTERPIECE: A TERRAPIN STATION > MORNING DEW. THIS IS ONE OF ONLY FIVE MORNING DEWS THE BAND PLAYED IN ALL OF 1977, WHICH ALONE MAKES THIS TAPE ESSENTIAL. THE PASSAIC MORNING DEW IS CONSIDERED 'RIGHT UP THERE WITH CORNELL'S' — THE KIND OF PERFORMANCE WHERE GARCIA'S VOICE REACHES FOR SOMETHING HE CAN'T QUITE PUT INTO WORDS AND THE GUITAR CARRIES THE REST. TERRAPIN STATION WAS STILL LESS THAN TWO MONTHS OLD AS A LIVE PIECE — EVERY PERFORMANCE SLIGHTLY DIFFERENT AS THE BAND WORKED OUT WHERE THE SONG WANTED TO GO. TOMORROW THE TOUR TAKES A DAY OFF. FRIDAY THEY CROSS THE HUDSON RIVER TO THE PALLADIUM FOR FIVE NIGHTS IN NEW YORK CITY.
1977-04-29
THE PALLADIUM ON EAST 14TH STREET, NEW YORK CITY
238w
APRIL 29, 1977 — THE PALLADIUM ON EAST 14TH STREET, NEW YORK CITY. FIRST OF FIVE NIGHTS. THE PALLADIUM WAS A 3,400-SEAT MOVIE PALACE FROM 1926 DESIGNED BY THOMAS W. LAMB — RECHRISTENED AND REOPENED AS A ROCK VENUE IN SEPTEMBER 1976 WITH THE BAND. THE ORIGINAL ARCHITECTURAL DETAILS WERE STILL VISIBLE ON THE CEILING BUT GRIMY WITH DECADES OF NEGLECT. KUNG FU MOVIES RAN BY DAY. THREE OR FOUR EVENINGS A WEEK IT BECAME THE ROOM WHERE THE RAMONES, TELEVISION, PATTI SMITH, AND THE DEAD ALL PLAYED ON DIFFERENT NIGHTS — THE ONLY VENUE IN NEW YORK WHERE PUNK AND PSYCHEDELIA PASSED THROUGH THE SAME DOORS. NEW YORK IN SPRING 1977: NEAR BANKRUPTCY, TWELVE PERCENT UNEMPLOYMENT, NEIGHBORHOODS HOLLOWED OUT. THE SON OF SAM HAD SHOT HIS FOURTH AND FIFTH VICTIMS THAT MONTH. STUDIO 54 HAD OPENED FOUR DAYS AGO. CBGB WAS FIFTEEN BLOCKS SOUTH ON THE BOWERY. THE WORLD TRADE CENTER TOWERS WERE SIX YEARS OLD. AN ATTENDEE WROTE: 'THIS ALSO HAPPENS TO BE THE SIXTH SHOW OF ARGUABLY THE FINEST GRATEFUL DEAD TOUR EVER.' THE HELP ON THE WAY > SLIPKNOT! > FRANKLIN'S TOWER OPENS THE SHOW AND SETS THE TONE — 'PHIL LEADS A SUPER GROOVY HELP WITH GREAT CHEMISTRY BETWEEN PHIL AND JERRY.' FIVE NIGHTS AT THE PALLADIUM WAS A RESIDENCY, NOT A TOUR STOP — THE DEAD PLAYING SAN FRANCISCO PSYCHEDELIC OPTIMISM IN THE MOST DANGEROUS, MOST CREATIVE, MOST DESPERATE CITY IN AMERICA.
1977-04-30
THE PALLADIUM, NEW YORK CITY
226w
APRIL 30, 1977 — THE PALLADIUM, NEW YORK CITY. SECOND OF FIVE NIGHTS. 'AFTER LAST NIGHT'S SHOW I'M TOTALLY STOKED FOR TONIGHT,' AN ATTENDEE RECALLED. 'THE PLACE IS VIBRATING IN ANTICIPATION.' THE BAND TOOK THE STAGE JUST FIFTEEN MINUTES AFTER THE STATED EIGHT O'CLOCK START — GIVING THE AUDIENCE BARELY ENOUGH TIME TO QUEUE FOR A BEER. ONE FAN WHO WAS A HIGH SCHOOL JUNIOR TOOK ACID FOR THE SECOND TIME IN HIS LIFE. 'I COULDN'T SAY IF IT WAS ACTUALLY GOOD,' HE ADMITTED, 'SINCE IT WAS THE SECOND TIME I TOOK ACID AND I WAS STILL A NOVICE DEADHEAD. A FEW DAYS LATER I SAW THE CORNELL SHOW AND WAS MORE ALERT, HAVING JUST A FEW TOKES. THAT WAS THE SHOW THAT MADE ME A DEADHEAD.' ANOTHER LISTENER: 'WOW, THIS SHOW IS THE BEST OF THE TOUR SO FAR. VOCALLY THE BAND IS MORE AND MORE ON POINT EVERY NIGHT. GREAT NIGHT FOR KEITH. THE GRATEFUL DEAD ARE SHOWING US THEY HAVE A WHOLE NEW ACT. THEY'RE LOVING THESE NEW TUNES.' THE SAINT STEPHEN WAS ONLY THE FOURTH SINCE ITS RETURN EARLIER THAT YEAR — A SONG THE BAND WAS STILL REDISCOVERING. OUTSIDE ON 14TH STREET THE NEIGHBORHOOD WAS PUNK KIDS AND DEADHEADS SHARING THE SIDEWALK. THE PALLADIUM IS NOW A DORMITORY FOR NEW YORK UNIVERSITY. THE LAMB-DESIGNED CEILING IS STILL THERE, ABOVE BUNK BEDS.
1977-05-04
THE PALLADIUM, NEW YORK CITY
190w
MAY 4, 1977 — THE PALLADIUM, NEW YORK CITY. THE LAST OF THE PALLADIUM NIGHTS. FIVE SHOWS IN SEVEN DAYS AT ONE VENUE — A RESIDENCY WHERE THE BAND AND AUDIENCE HAD BUILT SOMETHING TOGETHER OVER THE COURSE OF A WEEK. THE PALLADIUM WAS BIGGER THAN A CLUB BUT MORE INTIMATE THAN AN ARENA, WHICH IS WHY BANDS LOVED IT — YOU COULD FEEL THE ROOM RESPONDING. THE MISSISSIPPI HALF-STEP TAKES A FEW MINUTES TO SETTLE IN BUT ONCE IT DOES THE SOUND IS PRISTINE. THE BROWN EYED WOMEN IS CALLED OUT AS A HIGHLIGHT. BUT THE SHOW IS KNOWN FOR HOW IT ENDS: A PLAYING IN THE BAND THAT STARTS NORMALLY, THEN PLUNGES INTO DARK, MURKY, UNCERTAIN TERRITORY — 'REAL MURKY AND DARK, UNCERTAINTY ENSUES AS IT THROWS YOU FOR A LOOP,' ONE LISTENER WROTE. 'GREAT IMPROVISATION, KINDA LIKE THE 1974S.' IT RESOLVES INTO COMES A TIME — A SURPRISING AND MOVING TRANSITION. 'NOT A SONG I WOULD USUALLY COMMENT ON,' THE SAME LISTENER ADMITTED, 'BUT WOW.' TOMORROW THE TOUR HEADS NORTH — NEW HAVEN, THEN BOSTON, THEN A CAMPUS GYMNASIUM IN ITHACA. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 50.
1977-05-05
THE NEW HAVEN COLISEUM IN NEW HAVEN, CONNECTICUT
206w
MAY 5, 1977 — THE NEW HAVEN COLISEUM IN NEW HAVEN, CONNECTICUT. AN INVERTED PYRAMID DESIGNED BY ARCHITECT KEVIN ROCHE — THE PARKING GARAGE SAT ON TOP OF THE BUILDING BECAUSE THE HIGH WATER TABLE MADE UNDERGROUND PARKING IMPOSSIBLE. IT LOOKED LIKE A BRUTALIST SPACESHIP HAD LANDED IN DOWNTOWN NEW HAVEN. YALE UNIVERSITY WAS THREE BLOCKS AWAY. AN ATTENDEE WHO'D 'ROARED BACK UP I-91 FROM THE BIG APPLE WITH HAPPY MEMORIES OF THE FIVE NIGHTS' AT THE PALLADIUM ARRIVED WITH HIGH EXPECTATIONS — AND NOTED THAT THE NATURAL CONSEQUENCE OF THE BAND'S AIRPLANE GOING THE WRONG WAY EN ROUTE TO A SHOW WAS THAT 'STUFF DON'T WORK.' ANOTHER: 'THIS IS THE MONTH OF 1977. YOU CAN FIND AT LEAST EIGHT TO TEN SHOWS IN THE MONTH OF MAY THAT COULD BECOME ONE OF YOUR ALL-TIME FAVORITE GRATEFUL DEAD CONCERT RECORDINGS. THIS IS ONE OF THOSE SHOWS.' THE LAZY LIGHTNIN' > SUPPLICATION CRACKLES. THE SAINT STEPHEN MAKES ITS FIRST APPEARANCE OF THE SPRING TOUR. THIS IS THE FIRST OF THREE CONSECUTIVE NIGHTS IN THREE DIFFERENT CITIES — NEW HAVEN, BOSTON, CORNELL — THAT WOULD BECOME THE MOST FAMOUS STRETCH OF THE MOST FAMOUS TOUR. 1977-05-07
BOSTON GARDEN
226w
MAY 7, 1977 — BOSTON GARDEN. BUILT IN 1928 BY BOXING PROMOTER TEX RICKARD, SITTING DIRECTLY ABOVE NORTH STATION. NO AIR CONDITIONING. STEEL COLUMNS OBSTRUCTING VIEWS FROM HALF THE SEATS. LOW CEILINGS THAT TRAPPED SOUND AND HEAT UNTIL THE BUILDING FELT LIKE A KETTLE. PEELING PAINT, NARROW HALLWAYS, BATHROOMS LEGENDARY IN THEIR AWFULNESS. BOBBY ORR HAD FLOWN THROUGH THIS AIR, BILL RUSSELL HAD RAISED CHAMPIONSHIP BANNERS INTO THESE RAFTERS, AND WHEN PHIL LESH HIT A LOW NOTE THE PLASTER TREMBLED. THE GARDEN WAS EVERYTHING A MODERN ARENA IS NOT AND DEADHEADS LOVED IT. WBCN — CHARLES LAQUIDARA'S LEGENDARY MORNING SHOW — HAD BEEN TALKING UP THE CONCERT ALL WEEK. BOSTON IN SPRING 1977 WAS STILL BRUISED FROM THE BUSING CRISIS BUT THE COLLEGE-TOWN ENERGY WAS UNDIMINISHED. LEGAL SEAFOOD WAS A FISH MARKET IN CAMBRIDGE. IT WAS BILL KREUTZMANN'S BIRTHDAY. EQUIPMENT PROBLEMS PLAGUED THE FIRST SET WITH LONG BREAKS BETWEEN SONGS. A FRONT-ROW PHOTOGRAPHER CAUGHT GARCIA BREAKING INTO A BIG GRIN DURING MISSISSIPPI HALF-STEP — A VERSION ARGUED BY MANY TO BE THE BEST EVER PLAYED. ONE REVIEWER CALLED THIS 'THE FIRST NIGHT OF THE HOLY TRILOGY' — NEW HAVEN, BOSTON, CORNELL. SEATTLE SLEW HAD WON THE KENTUCKY DERBY THE DAY BEFORE. TOMORROW IS CORNELL. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977: GET SHOWN THE LIGHT BOX SET AND SEPARATELY ON VINYL FOR RECORD STORE DAY 2023.
1977-05-08
BARTON HALL AT CORNELL UNIVERSITY IN ITHACA, NEW YORK
423w
MAY 8, 1977 — BARTON HALL AT CORNELL UNIVERSITY IN ITHACA, NEW YORK. MOTHER'S DAY. THE MOST FAMOUS GRATEFUL DEAD RECORDING IN EXISTENCE. BARTON HALL IS A 1914 MILITARY DRILL HALL WITH A WOODEN FLOOR — A CAVERNOUS GYMNASIUM ON AN ISOLATED CAMPUS IN THE FINGER LAKES, WHERE YOU ARRIVE VIA ROUTE 13 THROUGH ROLLING FARMLAND AND GORGES SWOLLEN WITH SNOWMELT. EARLY MAY IN THE FINGER LAKES: THE APPLE ORCHARDS SHOULD HAVE BEEN IN FULL BLOSSOM BUT 1977 WAS NOT A NORMAL SPRING — THE BRUTAL WINTER HAD LINGERED AND THE TREES WERE ONLY BEGINNING TO LEAF OUT. TICKETS WERE SIX DOLLARS AND FIFTY CENTS. THE CORNELL CONCERT COMMISSION HAD BEEN $100,000 IN DEBT FROM PREVIOUS MONEY-LOSING SHOWS AND WAS OPERATING UNDER RESTRICTIONS FOLLOWING A 1973 DEEP PURPLE RIOT. FANS DROVE FROM AS FAR AS NORTH CAROLINA. HUNDREDS OF ADDITIONAL PEOPLE WERE ADMITTED BY SINGING, DANCING, TELLING JOKES, OR OFFERING GUITAR PICKS AND PEANUT BUTTER SANDWICHES. THE HALL WAS DENSELY PACKED AND STEAMING — T-SHIRTS CLINGING TO SWEATY BACKS, THE BAND ASKING THE AUDIENCE TO MOVE BACK. ONE ATTENDEE RECALLED BEING AT GARCIA'S FEET DURING A SOLO AND EXPERIENCING 'SHEER AWE.' HE WENT ON TO SEE THE BAND SEVENTY MORE TIMES. ANOTHER SAID THE SHOW FELT 'INTIMATE BECAUSE IT WAS OUR HOME TURF.' THE SCARLET BEGONIAS > FIRE ON THE MOUNTAIN THAT OPENS THE SECOND SET IS THE VERSION AGAINST WHICH ALL OTHERS ARE MEASURED. THE MORNING DEW — THIRTEEN MINUTES — IS WHAT THE BAND'S ARCHIVIST CALLED 'QUITE POSSIBLY ONE OF THE GRATEFUL DEAD'S BEST-EVER LIVE PERFORMANCES,' THE BAND 'SINGULARLY FOCUSED ON ACHIEVING THE CATHARTIC, FRENZIED RELEASE OF THE CLIMAX.' THE ENCORE: ONE MORE SATURDAY NIGHT — PLAYED ON A SUNDAY. SOMEONE SAID 'THANKS MOM.' WHEN THE AUDIENCE EMERGED FROM THE SWELTERING HALL THEY WALKED INTO A MAY SNOWSTORM — 'BIG BILLOWING FLAKES' SWIRLING IN THE PARKING LOT LIGHTS. 'ALMOST EVERYONE I'VE SPOKEN TO WHO WENT TO THE CONCERT MENTIONS IT.' ONLY THREE PHOTOGRAPHS OF THE CONCERT ARE KNOWN TO EXIST. A COLLEGE ROOMMATE PLAYED DISC ONE IN HIS JEEP AND A STUDENT WHO'D NEVER HEARD THE DEAD BECAME A FAN FROM THE LOSER ALONE. THE TAPE BECAME THE MOST COPIED CONCERT RECORDING IN HISTORY, PASSED HAND TO HAND THROUGH THE MAIL FOR DECADES. THE LIBRARY OF CONGRESS ADDED IT TO THE NATIONAL RECORDING REGISTRY IN 2012. BOB WEIR: 'I CAN'T REMEMBER THAT SPECIFICALLY. THE WHOLE TOUR WAS LIKE THAT FOR ME.' RELEASED AS A STANDALONE ALBUM IN 2017 AND AS PART OF THE MAY 1977: GET SHOWN THE LIGHT BOX SET.
1977-05-09
THE WAR MEMORIAL AUDITORIUM IN BUFFALO, NEW YORK
242w
MAY 9, 1977 — THE WAR MEMORIAL AUDITORIUM IN BUFFALO, NEW YORK. AN ART DECO WPA BUILDING FROM 1940 — BUFF-COLORED BRICK AND LIMESTONE WITH DECORATIVE METALWORK AND HIGH CEILINGS. COMMONLY CALLED 'THE AUD.' THE SABRES' FRENCH CONNECTION LINE HAD THRILLED CROWDS HERE. BUFFALO IN THE SPRING OF 1977 WAS STILL DIGGING OUT FROM THE BLIZZARD THAT HAD BURIED THE CITY IN JANUARY — FIVE PEOPLE DEAD, DRIFTS TO SECOND-STORY WINDOWS, THE NATIONAL GUARD CALLED IN. EVEN BY MAY THE TRAUMA WAS FRESH. THE NIGHT AFTER CORNELL. AN ATTENDEE 'WAS ENJOYING SPRING TIME IN GEORGIA WHEN A BUDDY GOING TO CORNELL SAID COME ON UP FOR THE DEAD. WE CAN HIT BARTON HALL AND THEN BUFFALO.' HE ALMOST DIDN'T GO — HAD A PAPER DUE AND A TEST. HE BOOKED A FLIGHT ANYWAY. THE HELP ON THE WAY > SLIPKNOT! > FRANKLIN'S TOWER THAT OPENS THE SECOND SET IS CONSIDERED BY MANY TO BE THE DEFINITIVE VERSION — OVER THIRTY MINUTES, TIGHTER AND MORE FEROCIOUS THAN CORNELL'S. THE ENCORE FEATURED 'ONE OF THE VERY BEST UNCLE JOHN'S BANDS THE DEAD EVER PLAYED LIVE.' SOME CONSIDER THIS SHOW 'OVERSHADOWED BY YEARS OF HYPE AND ADORATION FOR THE CORNELL PERFORMANCE.' THE BUILDING WAS DEMOLISHED IN 2009 DESPITE PRESERVATION EFFORTS — THE ART DECO DETAILING LOST FOREVER. THE ANCHOR BAR, WHERE BUFFALO WINGS WERE INVENTED IN 1964, WAS STILL A LOCAL SECRET. RELEASED AS PART OF THE MAY 1977: GET SHOWN THE LIGHT BOX SET.
