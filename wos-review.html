1974-02-22
WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO
166w
FEBRUARY 22, 1974 — WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO. THE FIRST SHOW OF THE YEAR, AND THE LAST BEFORE THE WALL OF SOUND WENT PUBLIC. THE PRE-WALL PA WAS ALREADY EXCEPTIONAL BUT NOTHING LIKE WHAT WAS COMING. THREE SONGS DEBUTED TONIGHT — SHIP OF FOOLS, U.S. BLUES, AND IT MUST HAVE BEEN THE ROSES — ALL THREE WOULD APPEAR ON FROM THE MARS HOTEL FOUR MONTHS LATER. BILL GRAHAM OPENED THE SHOW: "WHATEVER IS GOING ON WITH THE REST OF THE WORLD, WHETHER IT BE WARS, OR KIDNAPPING, OR CRIMES, THIS IS A PEACEFUL SUNDAY NIGHT WITH THE GRATEFUL DEAD." HE WASN'T BEING GENERIC. PATTY HEARST HAD BEEN KIDNAPPED FROM HER BERKELEY APARTMENT EIGHTEEN DAYS EARLIER. THE SYMBIONESE LIBERATION ARMY WAS DEMANDING MILLIONS IN FOOD DONATIONS. THE WATERGATE HEARINGS WERE GRINDING TOWARD IMPEACHMENT. THE ARAB OIL EMBARGO HAD QUADRUPLED GAS PRICES AND AMERICANS WERE WAITING IN HOURS-LONG LINES AT THE PUMP. AND HERE WAS BILL GRAHAM IN A CONVERTED ICE RINK REMINDING EVERYONE WHAT THEY'D COME FOR.
1974-02-23
WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO
156w
FEBRUARY 23, 1974 — WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO. NIGHT TWO. THE BAND HAD BEEN GRINDING THROUGH TAKES AT CBS COAST RECORDERS FOR THE MARS HOTEL ALBUM — THE STUDIO DEMANDING POLISH AND PRECISION, THE LIVE SHOW OFFERING FREEDOM. GARCIA'S GUITAR TONE IS FAT AND WARM, THE WOLF DIALED IN. KEITH GODCHAUX, WHO PHIL LESH SAID "TURNED INTO A FIRE-BREATHING DEMON" IN 1974, IS PARTICULARLY SHARP — "NOT ONLY FOLLOWING THE BAND EFFORTLESSLY THROUGH ALL OUR ENDLESSLY BIFURCATING HIGHWAYS AND BYWAYS, BUT GRABBING THE BALL AND RUNNING WITH IT." WITH KEITH, LESH WROTE, THE BAND "BECAME THE TURBOCHARGED TURN-ON-A-DIME GRATEFUL DEAD." BUT COCAINE USE WAS PEAKING ACROSS THE ORGANIZATION — BOBBY WEIR DESCRIBED THE CREW AS "DROWNING IN MOUNTAINS OF BLOW" AND CONSISTING OF PEOPLE "PAID LIKE EXECUTIVES FOR DOING BLUE-COLLAR WORK." THE OPERATION HAD BALLOONED UNTIL THE BAND WAS FORCED TO PLAY LARGER VENUES JUST TO COVER EXPENSES. A VICIOUS CYCLE THAT WOULD END IN OCTOBER.
1974-02-24
WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO
158w
FEBRUARY 24, 1974 — WINTERLAND ARENA, SAN FRANCISCO. THE FINAL NIGHT. THE SECOND SET INCLUDED A NEARLY THIRTY-MINUTE DARK STAR > MORNING DEW SEQUENCE — KEITH'S PIANO COMPLEMENTING GARCIA AT EVERY TURN, SO BEAUTIFUL THAT ONE REVIEWER CALLED IT "ONE OF THE MORE LIVELY DAVE'S PICKS TO FOCUS ON THE EARLY-SEVENTIES ERA DEAD." THE BAND HAD BEEN RECORDING FROM THE MARS HOTEL AT CBS COAST RECORDERS SINCE APRIL — DENNIS MCNALLY CALLED THE ALBUM "A SLICK-SOUNDING ALBUM OF STAND-ALONE SONGS. HIGHLY PROFESSIONAL, BUT NOT INSPIRED GRATEFUL DEAD." BILL KREUTZMANN: "THE STUDIO FELT CONTRIVED. IT COULDN'T OFFER THE FREEDOM OF PLAYING SOMETHING LIVE, NOR THE SATISFACTION." THE LIVE SHOW WAS WHERE THE MUSIC WAS REAL. THE STUDIO WAS THE OBLIGATION. AN MIT REVIEWER NOTED THE BAND HAD SHIFTED TOWARD "MORE GENERALLY LISTENABLE MUSIC" AT THE COST OF THEIR "ACID-ORIENTED, EMOTIONALLY CHARGED" HERITAGE. BUT LISTEN TO THIS DARK STAR AND TELL HIM HE'S RIGHT. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 13.
1974-03-23
COW PALACE, DALY CITY
234w
MARCH 23, 1974 — COW PALACE, DALY CITY. BILLED AS "THE SOUND TEST." THE PUBLIC DEBUT OF THE WALL OF SOUND — THE LARGEST CONCERT SOUND SYSTEM EVER BUILT. OVER 600 SPEAKERS STACKED THREE STORIES HIGH BEHIND THE BAND, 75 TONS, POWERED BY 48 MCINTOSH AMPLIFIERS DELIVERING 28,800 WATTS. EVERY INSTRUMENT HAD ITS OWN DEDICATED SPEAKER COLUMN. THE BAND HEARD EXACTLY WHAT THE AUDIENCE HEARD — NO STAGE MONITORS, NO SEPARATION BETWEEN PERFORMER AND LISTENER. THE COW PALACE WAS LITERALLY A LIVESTOCK PAVILION — THE CALIFORNIA STATE LIVESTOCK BUILDING, BUILT IN 1941, ITS FIRST EVENT THE WESTERN CLASSIC HOLSTEIN SHOW. TWO WEEKS LATER, PEARL HARBOR; THE GOVERNMENT REPURPOSED IT AS A MILITARY PROCESSING STATION. BY 1974 IT HELD 15,000 FOR CONCERTS. TONIGHT IT ALSO HELD THE DEBUT OF SCARLET BEGONIAS AND CASSIDY. A FAN TRIPPING ON LSD DESCRIBED THE SOUND: "THE MUSIC CAME FROM EVERY DIRECTION WITH PINPOINT CLARITY. IT WAS EVERYWHERE AND ANYWHERE ALL AT ONCE." DURING CHINA CAT SUNFLOWER, "WHEN THEY GET TO THE BRIDGE AND HIT THIS GIANT E CHORD, PHIL HIT A NOTE THAT CAUSED THE ENTIRE ROOM TO RATTLE." COURTENAY POLLOCK CREATED TIE-DYE SPEAKER FRONTS AND FLEW MANDALA BANNERS FROM THE SCAFFOLDING WITHOUT A HARNESS. A CREW MEMBER NAMED SPARKY HUNG IN A HAMMOCK AT THE TOP OF THE RIG DURING SHOWS. PHIL LESH COMPARED PLAYING THROUGH IT TO "PILOTING A FLYING SAUCER." RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 24.
1974-05-12
MACKAY STADIUM, UNIVERSITY OF NEVADA, RENO
172w
MAY 12, 1974 — MACKAY STADIUM, UNIVERSITY OF NEVADA, RENO. MOTHER'S DAY. AN OUTDOOR SHOW WHERE WIND WAS A SIGNIFICANT FACTOR — THE AUDIENCE WATCHED NERVOUSLY AS THE MASSIVE WALL OF SOUND SWAYED IN THE GUSTS. BUT THE SOUND DISPERSED CLEANLY ACROSS THE STADIUM DESPITE THE CONDITIONS. RENO IN 1974 WAS NOT LAS VEGAS — IT WAS GRITTIER, LOCALS-FIRST, THE KIND OF TOWN WHERE YOUR BARTENDER KNEW YOUR NAME AND YOU COULD GET A STEAK SPECIAL AFTER MIDNIGHT. THE NEIGHBORHOOD CASINOS HAD NICKEL SLOTS. THE UNIVERSITY EXISTED IN UNEASY TENSION WITH THE GAMBLING ECONOMY AROUND IT. ONE ATTENDEE WHO BECAME A PROFESSIONAL MUSICIAN CALLED THIS HIS ALL-TIME FAVORITE DEAD SHOW, PRAISING THE "AMAZING TRANSPARENCY OF THE WALL" — HE COULD HEAR DETAILS LIKE A LOOSE NUT ON BILLY'S KICK PEDAL FROM FAR IN THE BACK OF THE STADIUM. THE GAS CRISIS WAS REAL — DRIVING TO RENO FROM ANYWHERE MEANT CHECKING THE PUMP SITUATION BEFORE YOU LEFT. A COMPLETE RECORDING WAS INCLUDED IN THE FROM THE MARS HOTEL 50TH ANNIVERSARY REISSUE (2024).
1974-05-14
ADAMS FIELD HOUSE, UNIVERSITY OF MONTANA, MISSOULA
168w
MAY 14, 1974 — ADAMS FIELD HOUSE, UNIVERSITY OF MONTANA, MISSOULA. THE ONLY GRATEFUL DEAD CONCERT EVER PERFORMED IN THE STATE OF MONTANA. SEVEN THOUSAND PEOPLE FILLED THE FIELD HOUSE — A GYM WITH LAMINATED WOOD ARCHES, OPENED IN 1953. MISSOULA WAS A TIMBER AND LOGGING TOWN WRAPPED AROUND THE UNIVERSITY. THE BAND CAME PARTLY BECAUSE WRITER ED MCCLANAHAN WAS TEACHING AT THE UNIVERSITY AND HAD RECENTLY PUBLISHED "GREATFUL DEAD I HAVE KNOWN" IN PLAYBOY MAGAZINE. THE SHOW RAN FOUR HOURS — A SPRAWLING, GENEROUS SET. THE ENCORE IS REMEMBERED FOR THE PITCHER INCIDENT: A ROWDY ATTENDEE FROM THE ABER DAY KEGGER FLUNG A PLASTIC PITCHER AT THE STAGE. IT HIT BOB WEIR ON THE HEAD. ON THE TAPE, WHEN THE BAND COMES BACK OUT, YOU CAN HEAR WEIR SAY: "THANKS A HELL OF A LOT." THE WALL OF SOUND IN A COLLEGE FIELD HOUSE IN THE NORTHERN ROCKIES — SEVEN THOUSAND MILES AND A WORLD AWAY FROM WHERE IT WAS BUILT. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 9.
1974-05-17
P
153w
MAY 17, 1974 — P.N.E. COLISEUM, VANCOUVER, BRITISH COLUMBIA. PACIFIC COLISEUM ON THE PACIFIC NATIONAL EXHIBITION GROUNDS AT HASTINGS PARK — HOME OF THE NHL CANUCKS AND THE WHA BLAZERS. AN INDOOR HOCKEY ARENA HOSTING DEADHEADS AND THE WALL OF SOUND. THE TOUR MIRRORED THE 1973 PACIFIC NORTHWEST SEQUENCE — SAME VENUES, SAME DAYS OF THE WEEK, PUSHED BACK A MONTH. THIS SHOW FEATURED THE FIRST-EVER PERFORMANCE OF "MONEY MONEY" — A SONG THE BAND WOULD PLAY ONLY THREE TIMES BEFORE DROPPING IT PERMANENTLY. THREE PERFORMANCES AND GONE. THE KIND OF THING THAT HAPPENED WHEN A BAND WAS WRITING AND TESTING MATERIAL ON THE ROAD BETWEEN STUDIO SESSIONS FOR FROM THE MARS HOTEL — SOME SONGS STUCK AND SOME DIDN'T. SCARLET BEGONIAS WAS TWO MONTHS OLD AND ALREADY A KEEPER. MONEY MONEY WAS NOT. VANCOUVER IN 1974: A WEST COAST PORT CITY WITH MOUNTAINS AND RAIN, THE COUNTERCULTURE ALIVE IN KITSILANO AND GASTOWN.
1974-05-19
PORTLAND MEMORIAL COLISEUM
171w
MAY 19, 1974 — PORTLAND MEMORIAL COLISEUM. A MASTERPIECE OF MIDCENTURY MODERN ARCHITECTURE BY SKIDMORE, OWINGS AND MERRILL — DOUBLE THE SIZE OF A PORTLAND CITY BLOCK YET STANDING ON JUST FOUR COLUMNS. GLASS AND ALUMINUM EXTERIOR, NICKNAMED "THE GLASS PALACE." TWO BLACK GRANITE WALLS INSCRIBED WITH NAMES OF LOCAL WAR DEAD. THE TRUCKIN' JAM FROM THIS NIGHT IS CONSIDERED BY MANY DEADHEADS TO BE THE BEST MUSIC THE GRATEFUL DEAD EVER PLAYED. AFTER A SLOW, SWEET PEGGY-O, THE BAND LAUNCHED INTO TRUCKIN' WITH STRAIGHT-UP ROCK AND ROLL ENERGY, THEN TRANSITIONED INTO A MIND LEFT BODY JAM WITH GARCIA ON SLIDE GUITAR WITH A DISTORTED TONE, WEIR PLAYING STACCATO CHORDS, BEFORE THE WHOLE THING MORPHED INTO SPACE-JAZZ FUNK. ROLLING STONE CALLED IT AN "EPIC TRUCKIN' JAM." GERALD FORD WOULD WATCH A TRAIL BLAZERS GAME IN THIS BUILDING FIVE MONTHS LATER — THE FIRST SITTING PRESIDENT TO ATTEND AN NBA GAME. RELEASED AS A STANDALONE SIX-LP SET LIMITED TO 7,500 COPIES AND AS PART OF THE 19-CD PACIFIC NORTHWEST '73-'74 BOX SET.
1974-05-21
HEC EDMUNDSON PAVILION, UNIVERSITY OF WASHINGTON, SEATTLE
166w
MAY 21, 1974 — HEC EDMUNDSON PAVILION, UNIVERSITY OF WASHINGTON, SEATTLE. HOME OF THE FORTY-SEVEN-MINUTE PLAYING IN THE BAND — THE LONGEST CONTINUOUS GRATEFUL DEAD SONG EVER RECORDED. NOT A SUITE. NOT A SANDWICH WITH OTHER SONGS INSIDE. A SINGLE UNBROKEN PIECE OF MUSIC LASTING FORTY-SEVEN MINUTES. THE WALL OF SOUND'S 450 SPEAKERS BACKED THE STAGE IN THIS UNIVERSITY GYMNASIUM WHILE A BAND DECIDED THERE WAS NO REASON TO STOP — THAT THE TERRITORY THEY WERE EXPLORING HAD NO BOUNDARY THEY NEEDED TO RESPECT. SEATTLE IN 1974 WAS IN ECONOMIC CRISIS — BOEING HAD LAID OFF 60,000 WORKERS AND SOMEONE HAD PUT UP A BILLBOARD: "WILL THE LAST PERSON LEAVING SEATTLE TURN OUT THE LIGHTS?" THE TECH INDUSTRY HADN'T ARRIVED. THE CITY WAS CHEAP, GRAY, AND SLIGHTLY DESPERATE. THE DEAD PLAYED A GYM AND PRODUCED WHAT MAY BE THE SINGLE MOST AMBITIOUS PIECE OF IMPROVISED MUSIC IN ROCK HISTORY. RELEASED AS A STANDALONE VINYL LP ON RECORD STORE DAY BLACK FRIDAY 2018, LIMITED TO 7,400 COPIES.
1974-06-08
OAKLAND-ALAMEDA COUNTY COLISEUM STADIUM
197w
JUNE 8, 1974 — OAKLAND-ALAMEDA COUNTY COLISEUM STADIUM. THE FIRST BILL GRAHAM "DAY ON THE GREEN" — A FORMAT THAT WOULD DEFINE BAY AREA STADIUM CONCERTS FOR THE NEXT DECADE. COMMANDER CODY OPENED AND ASKED THE CROWD: "HOW COME I DON'T SEE MORE OF YOU HIPPIES AT BASEBALL GAMES?" THE A'S WERE THE REIGNING WORLD CHAMPIONS AND THIS WAS THEIR STADIUM. ABOUT THIRTY THOUSAND PEOPLE SPREAD ACROSS THE OUTFIELD GRASS WITH BLANKETS AND PICNICS IN A VENUE THAT HELD SIXTY THOUSAND. IT WAS PASTORAL, UNHURRIED. TICKETS WERE EIGHT FIFTY. THE BEACH BOYS WERE LATE — STUCK IN TRAFFIC FROM SAN FRANCISCO — AND THEIR CREW REFUSED TO USE THE WALL OF SOUND, INSISTING ON THEIR OWN PA. OWSLEY STANLEY WAS SO UPSET HE BROKE HIS ARM FALLING FROM THE SCAFFOLDING WHILE TAKING DOWN THEIR EQUIPMENT. THE DEAD SHORTENED THEIR SET TO ACCOMMODATE THE DELAY. THAT EVENING, GARCIA DROVE TO THE GREAT AMERICAN MUSIC HALL AND PLAYED ANOTHER FULL SHOW WITH MERL SAUNDERS. ONE FAN DESCRIBED A WAVE OF CLAPPING TRAVELING ACROSS THE STADIUM AT THE SPEED OF SOUND — "PRETTY INTENSE IN AN ALTERED STATE." THE AUD TAPES ARE SO GOOD THEY REGULARLY CIRCULATE MISLABELED AS SOUNDBOARD.
1974-06-16
IOWA STATE FAIRGROUNDS, DES MOINES
173w
JUNE 16, 1974 — IOWA STATE FAIRGROUNDS, DES MOINES. THE FIRST TIME THE WALL OF SOUND WAS HEARD OUTDOORS IN ITS FULL CONFIGURATION. IOWA LET FANS CAMP AT THE FAIRGROUNDS FOR FREE, WITH RUNNING WATER AND SHOWERS — A GENEROSITY ROOTED IN THE STATE'S AGRARIAN HOSPITALITY. THE HEART OF THE FAIR WAS "DEEPLY ROOTED IN AGRARIAN HERITAGE" — HOG-CALLING COMPETITIONS, FIDDLING CONTESTS, FIREWORKS. NOW THERE WERE DEADHEADS ON THE MIDWAY AND 75 TONS OF SPEAKERS RISING BEHIND THE STAGE AT A PLACE BUILT FOR LIVESTOCK JUDGING. THE GAS CRISIS WAS IN FULL EFFECT — THE ARAB OIL EMBARGO HAD QUADRUPLED PRICES AND THE NATIONAL SPEED LIMIT HAD BEEN DROPPED TO 55 MPH. DRIVING THE FOUR SEMI-TRUCKS FROM VENUE TO VENUE COST MORE EACH WEEK AS DIESEL PRICES CLIMBED. GERALD FORD'S "WHIP INFLATION NOW" BUTTONS WERE EVERYWHERE — ONE OF THE BIGGEST GOVERNMENT PR BLUNDERS IN HISTORY. THE STANDOUT TENNESSEE JED AND SPACIOUS TRUCKIN' JAMS SUGGESTED THE BAND WAS UNBOTHERED BY ANYTHING HAPPENING OUTSIDE THE FAIRGROUNDS. RELEASED AS ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 2, NUMBER 3.
1974-06-18
FREEDOM HALL, LOUISVILLE, KENTUCKY
153w
JUNE 18, 1974 — FREEDOM HALL, LOUISVILLE, KENTUCKY. THE STATE FAIR COMPLEX. SOME FANS CONSIDER THIS THE BEST SHOW OF THE ENTIRE YEAR — A CLAIM THAT HAS TO CLEAR A HIGH BAR IN 1974. GARCIA QUOTED "MY OLD KENTUCKY HOME" DURING HIS BEAT IT ON DOWN THE LINE SOLO — THE KIND OF REGIONAL NOD THAT HAPPENED WHEN GARCIA WAS PAYING ATTENTION TO WHERE HE WAS. THE OTHER ONE PRODUCED THE FIRST AND ONLY KNOWN "IT'S A SIN JAM" — AN IMPROVISED PASSAGE THAT EMERGED FROM NOWHERE, EXISTED FOR A FEW MINUTES, AND VANISHED. THE KIND OF MUSIC THAT ONLY HAPPENED LIVE, THAT COULD NEVER BE PLANNED OR REPEATED. LOUISVILLE IN JUNE 1974: HANK AARON HAD BROKEN BABE RUTH'S RECORD TEN WEEKS EARLIER, JUST DOWN THE ROAD IN ATLANTA. THE KENTUCKY DERBY WAS A MONTH PAST. THE HUMID AIR OF THE OHIO RIVER VALLEY. RELEASED AS ROAD TRIPS VOLUME 2, NUMBER 3.
1974-06-20
THE OMNI, ATLANTA
161w
JUNE 20, 1974 — THE OMNI, ATLANTA. BUILT OVER AN UNUSED RAILROAD GULCH IN DOWNTOWN ATLANTA, OPENED 1972, COST SEVENTEEN MILLION DOLLARS. ITS DISTINCTIVE COR-TEN STEEL ROOF — DESIGNED TO RUST PROTECTIVELY — NEVER STOPPED RUSTING IN ATLANTA'S HUMIDITY. EVENTUALLY HOLES APPEARED IN THE WALLS LARGE ENOUGH TO CLIMB THROUGH. TONIGHT SAW THE DEBUT OF SLIPKNOT! — THE MIDDLE PIECE OF WHAT WOULD BECOME THE HELP ON THE WAY > SLIPKNOT! > FRANKLIN'S TOWER SUITE. HANK AARON HAD BROKEN THE HOME RUN RECORD TEN WEEKS EARLIER, TEN MILES FROM THIS BUILDING, AFTER RECEIVING DEATH THREATS FOR MONTHS. VIN SCULLY'S CALL: "A BLACK MAN IS GETTING A STANDING OVATION IN THE DEEP SOUTH FOR BREAKING A RECORD OF AN ALL-TIME BASEBALL IDOL, AND IT IS A GREAT MOMENT FOR ALL OF US." THE ATLANTA THE DEAD WERE PLAYING IN WAS A CITY IN TRANSITION — MAYNARD JACKSON HAD BEEN ELECTED THE FIRST BLACK MAYOR OF A MAJOR SOUTHERN CITY THE PREVIOUS YEAR.
1974-06-22
JAI-ALAI FRONTON, MIAMI
163w
JUNE 22, 1974 — JAI-ALAI FRONTON, MIAMI. JAI ALAI IS A BASQUE SPORT WHERE PLAYERS HURL A BALL AT SPEEDS UP TO 188 MILES PER HOUR USING A CURVED WICKER BASKET STRAPPED TO THEIR ARM, AGAINST THREE WALLS OF A 176-FOOT COURT. THE MIAMI FRONTON WAS DUBBED THE SPORT'S "YANKEE STADIUM" — PEAK POPULARITY IN 1970S FLORIDA, 15,000 SPECTATORS FOR MATCHES, THE FRONT WALL CAGED IN FOR THE PELOTA. WHEN THE PLAYERS WERE OFF, ANY BAND COULD TAKE THE FLOOR. THE WALL OF SOUND IN A JAI ALAI PALACE — THE STRANGEST CONFIGURATION IT EVER SAW. SOUTH FLORIDA IN JUNE: FLAT, HOT, SUBTROPICAL, AFTERNOON THUNDERSTORMS OVER THE EVERGLADES. MIAMI WAS PRE-VICE, PRE-COCAINE COWBOYS. ONE LISTENER: "MY HEART AND MIND AND SOUL WAS ABSOLUTELY BLOWN AWAY TO THE POINT OF TEARS BY WHAT MUST BE THE BEST VERSION OF GREATEST STORY EVER TOLD I'VE EVER HEARD." THE CHINA CAT > I KNOW YOU RIDER "IS ONE OF THE BEST I'VE HEARD FROM ANY YEAR."
1974-06-23
JAI-ALAI FRONTON, MIAMI
133w
JUNE 23, 1974 — JAI-ALAI FRONTON, MIAMI. NIGHT TWO. DAVID LEMIEUX CALLED THIS "NOT ONLY ONE OF THE VERY BEST SHOWS FROM THIS OUTSTANDING YEAR, BUT ALSO ONE OF THE MOST INTERESTING AND UNIQUE." TONIGHT: THE FIRST LIVE PERFORMANCE OF SEASTONES — NED LAGIN ON COMPUTER-CONTROLLED ANALOG SYNTHESIZERS, PHIL LESH ON ELECTRONICALLY PROCESSED BASS. LAGIN WAS THE FIRST PERSON TO PERFORM LIVE WITH COMPUTERS ON STAGE. TWENTY-THREE SEASTONES PERFORMANCES WOULD HAPPEN IN 1974 — SOMETIMES AN ENTIRE SET, SOMETIMES SEGUING INTO PLAYING IN THE BAND OR DARK STAR. AUDIENCES WERE DIVIDED. REVIEWERS SOMETIMES WALKED OUT. ALSO TONIGHT: THE DEAD'S ONLY PERFORMANCE OF CHUCK BERRY'S LET IT ROCK — ONE AND DONE. THE COLLECTOR WHO ASSEMBLED THIS COLLECTION CHOSE THIS NIGHT ABOVE ALL THIRTY-SIX OTHERS AS HIS PICK. RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 34.
1974-06-26
PROVIDENCE CIVIC CENTER
125w
JUNE 26, 1974 — PROVIDENCE CIVIC CENTER. A BEER BARREL POLKA TEASE BETWEEN CHINA CAT SUNFLOWER AND I KNOW YOU RIDER — ABSURDIST MUSICAL HUMOR FROM A BAND IN A PLAYFUL MOOD. THEN TRUCKIN' > OTHER ONE JAM > SPANISH JAM > WHARF RAT — FOUR SONGS DISSOLVING INTO EACH OTHER, BOUNDARIES IRRELEVANT, THE BAND AT ITS MOST EXPLORATORY. ONE REVIEWER: "A HORRENDOUS RECORDING OF A GORGEOUS SHOW." THE SOUND FRUSTRATES BUT THE MUSIC REWARDS PATIENCE — WHICH IS TRUE OF 1974 IN GENERAL. PROVIDENCE WAS A WORKING-CLASS NEW ENGLAND CITY WITH AN ITALIAN QUARTER ON FEDERAL HILL, A STATE CAPITOL THAT NEVER QUITE LIVED UP TO ITS MARBLE DOME, AND A MAYOR WHO WOULD LATER GO TO PRISON. RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 12.
1974-06-28
BOSTON GARDEN
157w
JUNE 28, 1974 — BOSTON GARDEN. THE OLD BOSTON GARDEN — BUILT IN 1928 BY TEX RICKARD ABOVE NORTH STATION. NO AIR CONDITIONING. STEEL COLUMNS BLOCKING VIEWS. JUNE IN BOSTON WITHOUT AC MEANT THE BUILDING WAS A SAUNA — THE LOW CEILING TRAPPED HEAT AND SOUND UNTIL BOTH BECAME PHYSICAL FORCES. THE WEATHER REPORT SUITE > LET IT GROW WAS PARTICULARLY FIERY, AND THE DEAD UNLEASHED A THIRTY-MINUTE POST-LET IT GROW JAM OF "EPIC PROPORTIONS" THAT HINTED AT DARK STAR ALONG THE WAY. THE KIND OF EXTENDED IMPROVISATION THAT ONLY HAPPENED IN 1974 — WHEN THE BAND HAD THE WALL OF SOUND BEHIND THEM, NO REASON TO HURRY, AND THE CONVICTION THAT WHEREVER THE MUSIC WANTED TO GO WAS MORE IMPORTANT THAN WHERE THEY'D PLANNED TO TAKE IT. THE RED SOX WERE ON THEIR WAY TO THE PENNANT. THE CELTICS WERE IN A DOWN PERIOD BETWEEN DYNASTIES. WBCN WAS THE SOUNDTRACK. RELEASED AS DICK'S PICKS VOLUME 12.
1974-06-30
SPRINGFIELD CIVIC CENTER
126w
JUNE 30, 1974 — SPRINGFIELD CIVIC CENTER. THE LAST NIGHT OF A PUNISHING STRETCH — FOUR SHOWS IN FIVE DAYS FROM PROVIDENCE TO BOSTON TO SPRINGFIELD. THE FOUR SEMI-TRUCKS CARRYING THE WALL OF SOUND BURNED DIESEL WHILE GAS STATIONS RAN DRY AND THE NATIONAL SPEED LIMIT SAT AT 55. SPRINGFIELD — BIRTHPLACE OF BASKETBALL, BLUE-COLLAR INSURANCE CITY IN THE CONNECTICUT VALLEY — WAS A FAMILIAR DEAD STOP. BEAR STANLEY HAD DESIGNED THE SYSTEM TO BE PURE: EVERYONE IN THE ROOM HEARD THE SAME SOUND, WHETHER THE BAND WAS ON FIRE OR RUNNING ON FUMES. THE DEMOCRATIC SYSTEM — NO MONITORS, NO SEPARATION BETWEEN STAGE AND AUDIENCE — MEANT THE MUSIC WAS HONEST. THERE WAS NOWHERE TO HIDE. SOMETIMES THAT HONESTY PRODUCED ITS OWN KIND OF BEAUTY.
1974-07-19
SELLAND ARENA, FRESNO
176w
JULY 19, 1974 — SELLAND ARENA, FRESNO. KEITH GODCHAUX'S BIRTHDAY. THE DEAD IN FRESNO MEANT THE WALL OF SOUND IN THE SAN JOAQUIN VALLEY — THE MOST PRODUCTIVE AGRICULTURAL REGION IN THE WORLD, FLAT AND HOT AND DRY, WHERE COTTON PICKERS WORKED IN HUNDRED-DEGREE HEAT ON THE WESTSIDE. FRESNO WAS NOT GLAMOROUS. BUT IT SAT MIDWAY BETWEEN SAN FRANCISCO AND LOS ANGELES AND A LOT OF GREAT BANDS PLAYED HERE ON SCHOOL NIGHTS. THE CROWD WAS THIN — THE 6,500-SEAT ARENA WAS NOT FULL. IT HAD BEEN THREE WEEKS SINCE THE LAST SHOW AND THERE WERE MINOR LYRIC MISCUES. BUT THE THIRD SET IS SOMETHING ELSE: WEATHER REPORT SUITE INTO SPANISH JAM INTO EYES OF THE WORLD INTO CHINA DOLL — OVER AN HOUR OF CONTINUOUS MUSIC. ONE REVIEWER CALLED THE SPANISH JAM "SO HEAVY, SO THICK, QUITE SCARY FOR THE UNINITIATED." PHIL WAS "A BEAST, DROPPIN' BOMBS LIKE A MADMAN." A SEVENTEEN-YEAR-OLD WHO CAME ON A COUSIN'S SUGGESTION REALIZED DURING U.S. BLUES THAT "THIS WAS WHERE IT WAS AT." RELEASED AS DAVE'S PICKS VOLUME 17.
1974-07-25
INTERNATIONAL AMPHITHEATRE, CHICAGO
130w
JULY 25, 1974 — INTERNATIONAL AMPHITHEATRE, CHICAGO. 42ND AND HALSTED, ADJACENT TO THE UNION STOCK YARDS IN CANARYVILLE — YOU COULD SMELL THE STOCKYARDS ON THE WIND. THIS BUILDING HOSTED THE 1968 DEMOCRATIC NATIONAL CONVENTION — ANTI-WAR RIOTS ON MICHIGAN AVENUE WHILE HUMPHREY WAS NOMINATED INSIDE. SIX YEARS LATER THE DEAD PLAYED IT. THE HOUSE JUDICIARY COMMITTEE HAD VOTED TO RECOMMEND IMPEACHMENT TWO DAYS AGO. NIXON HAD FIFTEEN DAYS LEFT. U.S. BLUES IN A BUILDING THAT HAD HOSTED 1968'S RAGE, WHILE 1974'S DISGRACE REACHED ITS CONCLUSION. ALL IN THE FAMILY WAS NUMBER ONE ON TELEVISION. CHINATOWN AND BLAZING SADDLES WERE IN THEATERS. ONE LISTENER WAS "FIRST ATTRACTED TO THIS SHOW BECAUSE OF THE UNUSUAL SET LIST, FEATURING MANY PERSONAL FAVES." A TIGHT BROWN-EYED WOMEN. THE AMPHITHEATRE WAS DEMOLISHED IN 1999.
1974-07-27
ROANOKE CIVIC CENTER
138w
JULY 27, 1974 — ROANOKE CIVIC CENTER. THE SMALLEST CITY ON THE SUMMER TOUR — A RAILROAD TOWN IN THE BLUE RIDGE MOUNTAINS WHERE THE NORFOLK AND WESTERN HAD ITS HEADQUARTERS AND THE NEON STAR ON MILL MOUNTAIN WATCHED OVER THE VALLEY. THE DEAD PLAYED "AT A VERY HIGH LEVEL, BLASTING INTO BERTHA." A UNIQUE FIVE-MINUTE JAM ON THE U.S. BLUES MELODY — "NEVER HEARD BEFORE OR SINCE."
